### Attractiveness as Core Value

v3.2 explicitly frames attractiveness as the system's unifying value: "The core value is attractiveness. We as humans are driven on attraction as a core motivator and driver." Attractiveness is operationalized as three 1–10 scores (Physical, Mental/Emotional, Status). The claim is that attractiveness functions as a power-law feedback loop — attractive people, communities, and systems compound their value recursively. This connects the personality development layer, the governance layer (high-scoring leaders earn delegated votes), and the economic layer (SBC/CRC/ECC earnings reflect demonstrated value). For the operational mechanics see [[leviathan-framework]] and [[emotional-evolution-personality-system]].

### Recursive Scientific Methodology as Hume Operationalization

The Recursive Scientific Methodology (v3.2 Ch.1, Ch.4) is the direct operationalization of Hume's nominalism: it combines inductive (data-driven) and deductive (model-driven) approaches with observers embedded inside the experimental frame, rejecting value-neutral science. Feedback loops refine hypotheses empirically. This is the epistemological core that ties the governance, development, and ecosystem systems together — every claim must be testable, every outcome logged, every metric recursively improved.
