# Ratchet 183: executable base MSS and whole-manifold feedback

Pack 183 repairs the missing beginning of the prior campaign.  It proposes a
finite set of base mathematical structures, simulates every one on the same
source-derived packets, compares them without a scalar score, nests every
current survivor under every declared nesting form, and then loops back after
entropy geometry arrives.

The important result is not one final winner.  It is an open six-candidate
frontier:

- base presentation: finite relation, finite automaton, or classical distribution;
- nesting presentation: complete pairwise coupling or a direct ternary relation;
- entropy geometry: Shannon potential with its Fisher Hessian metric.

The classical distribution was not on the base frontier.  It re-entered only
after the geometry layer made native scalar structure relevant.  That is the
intended upward/downward feedback behavior of the Ratchet.

Nothing here is called the absolute MSS.  The proposal grammar, projections,
requirements, and presumption coordinates are finite and declared.  Every
current winner is therefore a default among candidates compared so far.
