## 1. The ring checkerboard and nested Hopf construction are the literal GCM

The finite ring checkerboard and nested Hopf structures are the proposed
geometry of the entropic constraint manifold itself. They are not a picture
drawn over an otherwise unrelated Bloch carrier.

The intended object is recursively nested. A layer constrains internal layers;
order of nesting matters; and the operator/entropy ratchet and the geometric
constraint ratchet are two views of one construction. The ring-checkerboard
grammar supplies finite cells, discrete phases, recursive attachments, parity,
and alternative flat, curved, spherical, and spinning presentations. The Hopf
and spinor grammar supplies fibers, chirality, inner/outer organization,
holonomy, and higher-dimensional nesting candidates.

The owner's raw instruction was to use the ring checkerboard and nested Hopf
tori, with a Bloch sphere only as an alternate backup, and to run all seven axes
on the actual geometry. Therefore:

```text
literal finite ring/Hopf GCM  >  Bloch/33-cell fallback projection
```

A finite graph, cellular automaton, Bloch chart, spinor chart, or QCA may be a
useful chart or executable projection of the GCM. No such projection becomes
the whole manifold merely because it is easier to simulate.

## 2. “Shell” does not mean only a concentric two-sphere

A shell is a finite boundary, leaf, stratum, or nested constraint surface of
the GCM. An ordinary spherical shell may be one readout or low-dimensional
projection. It is not the complete ontology.

The hierarchy may pass through `S2` and `S3` and may contain intermediary
steps in either direction. Existing repository work on the finite division-

```text
S1 -> S3 -> S2
S3 -> S7 -> S4
S7 -> S15 -> S8
```

Those rungs are not hereby declared the final cosmological geometry. They show
why a summary that stops at `S3`, or replaces the GCM with concentric `S2`
shells, is an unauthorized narrowing. The exact weld among ring-checkerboard
depth, Hopf rung, spinor terrain, and physical shell remains open work.

## 3. The `i/j/k` structure

### `i`: global size/order scalar

`i` is the owner's global universe-size, expansion, shell-order, and universal-
clock candidate. It is distinct from local experienced or coordinate time
`t`. Local clocks may run differently while a global size/capacity/order
readout is proposed to remain mutually measurable in principle.

No current formula has earned identity with `i`. Entropy, area, correlation
extent, mutual information, coherent information, path evidence, or another
finite capacity measure may contribute to an operational realization, but none
may be installed by renaming it `i`. In particular, `i` is not established by
an engineered `ga0` constant, a static `phi0`, or monotonicity of a generic
information measure.

### `j/k`: finite future-possibility dimensions

`j` and `k` are the two extra future-possibility/time dimensions of the shell
field: the `j/k` fuzz. They encode the finite structured continuation space at
a present boundary. They are not automatically identical to quaternion or
octonion basis symbols with the same names, and the exact map remains open.

The model is finitist. It does not require infinitely many paths. A bounded
history registry, finite ring phases, finite cells, and finite admissible
continuations are sufficient in principle. But `j/k` fuzz is not exhausted by
counting outgoing graph edges. Its phase, order, interference, equivalence
classes, geometry, and constraint survival must remain available.

### Static fuzz versus ordered history

Pure independent static from frame to frame contains no persistent relation and
therefore no internal temporal order. Time/order appears when patterns,
correlations, entanglement, or records persist across the finite frames. A
future implementation must distinguish:

- uncorrelated static fuzz;
- coherent but unresolved alternatives;
- correlated or entangled continuation structure;
- recorded/decohered alternatives;
- classical mixtures.

Collapsing these cases into one random branch generator loses the model's
central distinction.

## 4. Many possible futures literally create the present

This is an ontological statement inside the owner model, not a metaphor for an
observer's ignorance. The present is constituted by the finite set of
admissible possible futures and their constraint relations. The possibilities
converge on a present; the model does not require a classical tree in which one
already-real present causes increasingly numerous future branches.

“Retrocausality” is an available name for this view, but it can mislead if it
implies a signal travelling from a later classical time to an earlier one. The
deeper claim is noncausal: the global finite history object and its boundary
constraints are prior to any fundamental forward/backward causal carrier.

The owner notation `KE iff PE` belongs to this direct-owner hypothesis layer.
It must be preserved verbatim until its operands and operational test are bound
by a source-qualified specification; an assistant must not guess an expansion
and then promote that guess.

### Minimum finite coherent-history semantics

For a finite outcome or terminal equivalence class `y`, let
`H_y` be its finite unresolved history registry. A genuine coherent construction
must combine alternatives before applying the outcome probability rule:

```math
C_y = \sum_{h \in H_y} A_h,
\qquad
p(y) = \operatorname{Tr}(E_y C_y \rho C_y^\dagger).
```

This contains cross-history terms

```math
\operatorname{Tr}(E_y A_h \rho A_{h'}^\dagger),
\qquad h \ne h'.
```

A finite Dirac/Feynman candidate compatible with the current architecture is

```math
A_{h,b}
= w_{h,b} e^{i\Theta_r[h,b]}
  \bigl(U_{n,b_n}\cdots U_{1,b_1}\bigr),
```

where `h` is a finite ordered history, `b` is explicit bracketing, and
`Theta_r` is an as-yet-uncommitted finite phase functional. Explicit bracketing
is required if nonassociative structure becomes load-bearing. A continuum,
metric, Lagrangian, classical action, or `hbar` must not be presumed at this
rung.

The recorded/decohered limit is different:

```math
p_{\mathrm{recorded}}(y)
= \sum_{h \in H_y}
  \operatorname{Tr}(E_y A_h \rho A_h^\dagger).
```

That expression has histories but no interference between distinct histories.
It cannot stand in for the owner's literal many-futures construction unless a
record or decoherence operation has been explicitly introduced.

## 5. Nonfundamental causality does not require an FTL carrier

The owner's Axis 0 program is intended to explain why entanglement and the
global constraint structure require neither a faster-than-light message nor a
message travelling backward in time. If reality is globally constrained by a
finite history ensemble, spatially separated correlations need not be produced
by a carrier propagating between the measurements.

The model must still satisfy operational no-signalling. For settings `x,y` and
outcomes `a,b`, a successful realization must recover conditions such as

```math
\sum_b p(a,b\mid x,y) = p(a\mid x),
\qquad
\sum_a p(a,b\mid x,y) = p(b\mid y).
```

These conditions prohibit controllable information transmission through the
correlation. They do not, by themselves, prove that the underlying ontology is
forward-causal. In this model:

```text
global constraint/correlation != propagating signal
no-signalling                != proof of fundamental causality
retrocausal vocabulary       != backward-in-time message carrier
```

For entanglement, merely asserting this distinction is not enough. A finite
realization must derive normalized probabilities, reproduce the intended Bell-
type correlations, preserve no-signalling under setting changes, and expose a
control in which removing cross-history coherence changes the correlations.

## 6. Axis 0 is a dynamic response polarity on the literal GCM

Axis 0 measures allostasis versus homeostasis. It is not FEP, although FEP may
be one later comparison language. It is not a static entropy value or a label
attached to a cell.

- **Allostatic:** a perturbation opens, spreads, explores, or sustains a broader
  structured continuation ensemble, boundary, shell, or basin response.
- **Homeostatic:** a perturbation is damped, bound, compressed, or reconverges
  toward the control ensemble, boundary, shell, or basin.

The polarity must be measured dynamically on the same state object that
carries the literal GCM and coherent finite histories:

```text
prepare -> perturb -> evolve the finite coherent ensemble
        -> recompute shells, continuation structure, and typed entropy
        -> compare with an unperturbed control over a finite window
        -> classify opening/spread versus binding/reconvergence
```

A minimal readout may compare changes in continuation support and coherence,
shell/boundary extent, basin occupancy, state-derived typed entropy, and
distance from reconvergence. The exact scalar or vector is open. The sign must
not be fitted to an installed label vector.

Required controls include zero perturbation, order reversal or shuffle,
coherent histories, recorded histories, phase scrambling, classical mixture,
single path, commuting dynamics, and nonassociative bracketing where enabled.
Axis 0 is not earned unless at least one real perturbation changes a dynamic
readout and the relevant controls can kill or alter that response.

## 9. Model status versus proof status

The following distinctions are mandatory:

1. **Owner ontology:** the statements in this correction specify the model the
   owner intends. They must be represented faithfully even when unproved.
2. **Mathematical proposal:** equations and carrier choices are candidate
   realizations until their objects, maps, and controls are explicit.
3. **Executable witness:** a passing finite simulation establishes only the
   bounded distinction actually computed.
4. **Scientific model:** comparison with standard quantum theory, relativity,
   cosmology, and experimental data requires separate derivations and tests.
5. **Empirical admission:** no file in this archive currently establishes that
   causality is unreal, that many futures physically constitute the present,
   that the proposed universal clock exists, or that the gravity model is true.

Unproved does not mean optional, metaphorical, or available for replacement.
The correct workflow is to preserve the hypothesis exactly, construct the
smallest faithful finite realization, design controls that distinguish it from
causal, incoherent, classical, and lower-dimensional alternatives, and then let
the evidence raise or lower its status.
