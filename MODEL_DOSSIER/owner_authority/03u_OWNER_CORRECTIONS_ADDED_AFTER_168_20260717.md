# Owner corrections added after the 168 source snapshot

These statements are retained as high-value owner wording. They narrow the
interpretation of the older source materials; they do not turn the following
mathematical proposals into established facts.

> flux is a property of each left or right Weyl spinor, or whatever class of
> spinor-like thing has a left or right version. Terrains are subspaces in each
> of those left or right spinors. Each has distinct topological spaces inside
> it, with smooth transitions between those stages.

> Flux, geometry, entropy are all one on these things.

> Flux is something deeply nested. Spinors are far to the outside, or much
> earlier in the nesting process. Terrains are much later. The exact layers and
> their order are not canon; the important idea is that orders of nesting and
> constraint produce different mathematics.

> The system has an entropy gradient from the start. It pushes exploration and
> finds every escape path. The manifold has to ratchet to close that escape.
> The 16 unique stages, if they emerge, are the culmination of distinct ways
> entropy can escape being contained. Nonassociativity may be another subtle
> escape degree.

> The 64 states have not been worked out. The hypothesis of four sub-engine
> stages is unfounded and must compete with other explanations.

The reconciliation reads the first two statements together: local chiral
spinors may supply a signed transport capacity, while a measurable flux needs
the nested geometry/loops/shell relations that make transport holonomy
operational. This is a candidate mathematical reading, not a replacement of
the owner wording.
