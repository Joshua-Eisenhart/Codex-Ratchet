# Root Ratchet kernel

The Ratchet is an agnostic finite mathematical process. It is not the owner's
physics model, the candidate grammar, a theorem prover, or Leviathan.

At tick `r`, freeze:

\[
K_r=(D_r,G_r,\Pi_r,\{\preceq_i\},C_r,H_r).
\]

- \(D_r\): finite typed distinction/continuation records currently available;
- \(G_r\): the replaceable finite exploration grammar;
- \(\Pi_r\): executable probes and measurements;
- \(\preceq_i\): explicit, plural presumption orders;
- \(C_r\): frozen controls and claim ceilings; and
- \(H_r\): immutable prior receipts, failures, and retained context.

The tick is:

\[
P_r=G_r(D_r,H_r),
\qquad
M_r(p)=\Pi_r(p;D_r),
\]

\[
A_r=\{p\in P_r:C_r(p,M_r(p))\},
\]

\[
F_{r,i}=\operatorname{Min}_{\preceq_i}(A_r),
\qquad
F_r=\{F_{r,i}\}_i.
\]

Incomparable minima remain alive. No hidden scalar chooses one ontology.

Held-out records, controls, and continuation probes generate a residual:

\[
\Delta_r=
\operatorname{Counterexamples}(F_r)
\cup
\operatorname{Unresolved}(F_r)
\cup
\operatorname{ContinuationMismatch}(F_r).
\]

The next obligation must be compiled from that measured residual:

\[
O_{r+1}=\operatorname{Compile}(\Delta_r,C_r,H_r).
\]

If \(\Delta_r\) supplies no new finite obligation, the process holds. It does
not manufacture a next step to keep a narrative moving.

The residual is retained as a vector, not collapsed into one search score:

\[
\Delta_r=(c_r,k_r,u_r,g_r,\ldots),
\]

with continuation conflicts, held-out kills, survivor underdetermination, and
source-runtime gaps kept distinguishable. Fair execution ensures that every
unresolved component remains live. A broad proposal registry may answer these
components, but it cannot alter their measurement or select its own success.

When the context fingerprint materially changes, every parked candidate is
eligible for re-offer. Selective execution may be dependency-aware, but
eligibility is universal. The full boundary is then recomputed; old summary
statistics are not treated as the new raw boundary.

## The pawl

The Ratchet's retained direction is carried by receipts and exclusions, not by
forcing survivor sets to shrink monotonically. The pawl requires:

- no admitted claim without an executable witness and its controls;
- no silent weakening of a gate, grammar, bound, or claim ceiling;
- no erasure of failed candidates or countermodels;
- no source-size weighting disguised as recurrence;
- no selector access to source names or owner-hypothesis labels;
- re-evaluation after a material context change; and
- a deletion/countermodel witness for every claimed load-bearing constraint.

Candidates may return from Purgatory and prior winners may fail. What cannot
move backward silently is the record of what was tested, under which frozen
context, and why its status changed.

## Nested constraint

The kernel permits nested candidate structures. For a candidate tower,

\[
\mathcal T_r=
\{(x_0,\ldots,x_L):
\bigwedge_i C_i(x_{\le i})\land
\bigwedge_i R_i(x_i,x_{i+1})\land H_r(x_{\le L})\}.
\]

The survivor at a layer is a projection of the complete compatible tower,

\[
X_{i,r}^{\star}=\pi_i(\mathcal T_r),
\]

so enclosing layers constrain their contents and inner extendability constrains
which enclosing layers survive. This is a kernel capability, not an admission
of any particular shell, spinor, Hopf, engine, or cosmological hypothesis.
