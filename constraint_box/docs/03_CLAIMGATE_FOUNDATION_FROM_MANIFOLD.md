# Requirements flowing back from the manifold goal

The attached manifold discussion is treated as mixed material: owner
constraints, mathematical candidates, implementation ideas, model failures,
and speculative physical interpretations. ConstraintBox takes generic
requirements from it without admitting its scientific conclusions.

## Foundation fixture

The current finite fixture links several candidate readings without claiming
their equivalence:

\[
H_0(\mathcal H)=\log_2|\mathcal H|,
\qquad
S_0(\rho)=\log_2\operatorname{rank}\rho,
\]

\[
S_1(\rho)=-\operatorname{Tr}(\rho\log_2\rho),
\qquad
\Delta_\Pi S=S_1(\Pi\rho)-S_1(\rho),
\]

where \(\Pi\) removes the declared off-diagonal terms. The fixture asks
stdlib, NumPy, JAX, and Quimb to compute bounded aspects independently. Equal
numbers on this fixture establish fixture parity only.

## Material deliberately not promoted into the kernel

- cosmological identifications of capacity with dark matter or dark energy;
- claims that constraint relaxation solves Millennium Prize problems;
- claims of oracle behavior or quantum advantage;
- claims that one carrier is forced by nature;
- a fixed total ordering of every proposed manifold branch;
- claims that a lowest-energy state is automatically the scientifically
  correct object;
- metaphors about engines, cognition, or Jungian functions inside formal
  object names or equations.

These may be retained as hypotheses with bridge assumptions and falsifiers.
They are not generic control-plane rules.
