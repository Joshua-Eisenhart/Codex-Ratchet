# Time-first manifold foundation seed

The first CB-side manifold object is finite and typed. The seed is a testable
notation for hypotheses from the wiki; it is not a canonical manifold or a
physics result.

## Finite carrier and capacity

Let `S_t` be an explicitly enumerated finite support at tick `t`, with

\[
  W_t = |S_t| > 0, \qquad K_t = \log_2 W_t, \qquad
  \Delta K_t = K_{t+1}-K_t .
\]

`K` is a Hartley-style support capacity: a measurement of finite
distinguishability, not thermodynamic entropy. A positive `\Delta K` is a
declared opening gradient in this seed, not a derived law of nature.

The object labelled `Z` is a typed finite-support boundary/root. It is not the
scalar zero of an algebra. Calling a label “magnitude of zero” does not by
itself define a norm, measure, signed quantity, or physical vacuum; those need
separate axioms and witnesses.

## Two order words at the first step

The seed exposes two typed compositions on the same finite witness domain:

\[
  L = B_L\!\circ O_L, \qquad R = O_R\!\circ B_R, \qquad L(S_0)\ne R(S_0).
\]

`open` and `bind` are operation labels, not yet physical processes. The order
gap is the minimal finite witness for a pair of distinct engine orderings. It
does not establish chirality; an oriented/chiral interpretation requires an
explicit symmetry group, action, and non-invariance witness.

## Constraint layers

The current seed orders constraints as:

1. **C0 — finitude:** finite carrier, finite support, and finite map domains.
2. **C1 — time opening/binding:** adjacent finite supports and measured
   capacity deltas; the two order words are present at the first time step.
3. **C2 — noncommutation:** the typed compositions are order-distinct.
4. **C3 — nonassociativity (candidate):** parenthesization becomes a new
   constraint only after an explicit common codomain and associator witness are
   supplied.

Each later layer is a restriction or deformation of the admissible relational
object. “Geometry” currently means the support, relations, maps, and allowed
compositions. “Entropy” currently means the declared capacity readout `K` and
its finite differences. The bridge between those notions is a hypothesis to
test, not an implicit identification.

The CB validator enforces the type/shape/order checks, recomputes `K` and
`\Delta K`, and emits a scratch diagnostic receipt. It intentionally leaves
canonical ordering, physical time, chirality, thermodynamic entropy,
nonassociative algebra, and CR truth outside its claim ceiling.
