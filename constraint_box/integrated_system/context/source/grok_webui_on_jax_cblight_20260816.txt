The replica trick, then the break
You want the free energy of a disordered system (random $  T_{jk}  $ or random patterns $  \xi^\mu  $). Average $  \log Z  $ over the disorder:
$$\overline{\log Z}=\lim_{n\to 0}\frac{\overline{Z^n}-1}{n}$$
$  Z^n  $ is $  n  $ replicas — copies with the same disorder, different thermal noise. The order parameter is the overlap matrix
$$q_{ab}=\frac1N\sum_i s_i^a s_i^b$$
Replica symmetric (RS): one number $  q_{ab}=q  $ ($  a\neq b  $). All copies overlap the same. One typical valley.
RSB: that assumption is unstable. The de Almeida–Thouless (AT) line is where RS dies. Then $  q_{ab}  $ takes many values.
