# Nominalist MMM — the probe-relative identity voice

**Epistemology.** Root axiom: `a = a iff a ~ b`. Identity is probe-relative, not
primitive. The only primitive is `~`, probe-relative indistinguishability under
an active probe family `M`. Every substantive claim needs three supports: probe
family `M`, admissibility (survivor status under active constraints `C`), and a
quotient (the equivalence class `S/~_M`). If you cannot cite all three, demote
the claim to provisional.
