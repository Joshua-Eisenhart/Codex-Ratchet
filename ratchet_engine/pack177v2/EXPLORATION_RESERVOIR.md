# Open mathematical exploration reservoir

This is a proposal reservoir, not a rank list or admission ledger. The binding
machine-readable registry is now `fuel/FUEL_REGISTRY.json`; the executed
unranked projection is `receipts/proposal_fuel_projection.json`. An entry may
be delayed by resources, but it may not disappear because it was absent from a
recent prompt or a narrow successful calibration.

The root process should compete families at multiple presumption levels:

1. finite relations, partial maps, total maps, automata, and transducers;
2. quotient/refinement lattices and continuation-equivalence structures;
3. local constraint hypergraphs, tiling/gluing systems, presheaves, sheaves,
   limits, and complete sections;
4. finite ordered and bracketed histories, coherent sums, process matrices,
   and quantum comb-like memory structures;
5. spinor, Clifford, division-algebra, and nonassociative carriers when their
   operational licenses are present;
6. graphs, oriented cell complexes, discrete connections, holonomy, and
   spectral/Dirac constructions as candidate emergent geometry; and
7. predictive representation learners, including JEPA-like methods retooled so
   that operational identity and plural continuation come before a fixed
   causal predictor.

A JEPA-like lane may propose a quotient by shared predictive continuation:

\[
x\sim_h y
\iff
\Lambda_h(x)\sim_{\Pi}\Lambda_h(y),
\]

rather than assume one vector target caused by one past state. Its learned
embedding is a computational proposal; held-out probes and exact finite
controls determine whether the quotient is retained.

Owner hypotheses remain separate high-value seeds and sealed comparisons.
They may guide which typed lanes are worth proposing without becoming selector
features or desired answers.

See `OWNER_HYPOTHESIS_FUEL_MAP.md` for their actual content. The former one-line
summary was not sufficient preservation.
