# Fuel execution contract

## Binding separation

1. The Root Ratchet compiles obligations from measured residuals.
2. The exploration registry proposes typed constructions capable of answering
   those obligations.
3. Owner hypotheses seed high-value lanes and sealed comparisons.
4. Leviathan schedules, events, resumes, graphs, and receipts execution.
5. Only source-native mathematical measurements and frozen controls move a
   frontier.

No layer may impersonate another.

## Executed local compiler

Run:

```text
python3 -B source/compile_fuel_obligations.py --self-test
python3 -B source/compile_fuel_obligations.py --run
python3 -B source/compile_fuel_obligations.py --validate
```

It must produce:

- `receipts/fuel_obligations.json`: one anonymous residual vector and typed
  next obligations per source;
- `receipts/fuel_schedule_projection.json`: a source-fair round-robin
  logistical queue for native Leviathan; and
- `receipts/proposal_fuel_projection.json`: every enabled registry entry,
  unranked and still bound to its evidence license.

## Residual-to-obligation rules

- A full-context continuation conflict requires preservation of the relation,
  an independently measured coordinate or longer history, and a finite
  transducer/history proposal. Richer carriers may not be fabricated from a
  family label.
- Multiple deterministic completions require an adaptive held-out context that
  distinguishes survivors. The compiler lists exact Boolean contexts where
  current ANF survivors disagree.
- A held-out kill creates a Purgatory receipt and universal eligibility after a
  material context change.
- A source-runtime error creates a repair-and-rerun obligation. It does not
  convert a returned projection into admitted source science.
- Even a unique completion remains subject to deletion, perturbation, and new
  continuation controls.

## Fair scheduling

The queue is round-robin across anonymous sources. It contains every unresolved
obligation and no epistemic score. A stable hash may break logistical ties but
is forbidden from affecting admission or MSS.

Proposal entries are also all retained. Resource limits may delay execution,
but delayed entries remain visible and eligible. Saliency, recent conversation,
donor size, owner-label match, and raw row count are forbidden priorities.

## Native Desktop continuation

Desktop should ingest both queues through the existing Leviathan estate in an
isolated worktree. Native discovery, invocation, start/hold/complete/failure
events, graph ingestion, and resume must be receipted. It should then route at
least one source obligation to an existing simulation/source interface and
recompute the affected frontier.

The source-balanced ANF census is a calibration inside this larger process. It
must never again replace the process's fuel ecology.
