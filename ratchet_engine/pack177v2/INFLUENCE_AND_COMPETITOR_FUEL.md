# Influence, competitor, and instrument fuel

These are construction sources and rivals, never authorities. Each must expose
its installed assumptions and run on a shared finite task before it can affect
a claim path.

- **Feynman/Dirac:** coherent finite ordered histories, phase, checkerboard,
  and spinor constructions. Compete recorded mixtures, phase-scrambled sums,
  classical paths, alternative finite carriers, and no-signalling controls.
- **Penrose/tiling/local-to-global mathematics:** finite matching rules,
  aperiodic organization, noncentral descriptions, substitution systems, and
  extension spaces. Compete Euclidean embedding against abstract adjacency,
  sheaf/gluing, and other constraint hypergraphs.
- **Spinor and Clifford geometry:** chirality, double covers, holonomy, and
  bilinear generation of vectors/tensors. Compete the quadratic-form/Clifford
  assumptions and compare against register, tensor, exterior, and categorical
  presentations.
- **Tensor networks, spin networks, group-field and spin-foam constructions:**
  useful discrete-entanglement/geometry execution families. Factorization,
  bond dimensions, graph choice, gauge data, and continuum interpretation must
  remain visible.
- **Noncommutative and spectral geometry:** candidate routes from algebra,
  Dirac spectra, and connections to effective geometry, gauge structure, and
  gravity. Compete finite incidence, graph, and nonmetric alternatives.
- **Division and exceptional algebra:** quaternionic, octonionic, Jordan,
  Clifford, triality, `G2`, `Spin(8)`, `F4`, and related candidates. Separate
  each algebraic atom and demand a load-bearing source witness before climbing.
- **Friston/FEP and active inference:** prediction, action, persistence, and
  viability. Compete probability, classical state, Markov blanket, causal
  direction, and objective choices.
- **JEPA and predictive world models:** predictive equivalence, object
  formation, and action-conditioned continuation. Retool fixed vector latents
  and one-way causal targets to the project's plural operational constraints.
- **Michael Levin:** multi-scale memory, morphogenesis, basin organization,
  intervention, and goal-directedness test cases. Compete substrate-specific
  mechanisms and nonbiological constructions.
- **Lee Cronin:** assembly, construction paths, selection, and chemistry-facing
  tests. Make primitive parts, assembly index, and causal production framing
  explicit; compete bracket-preserving measures.
- **Stephen Wolfram:** multiway rewriting and computational-universe testbeds.
  Compete fundamental update direction, graph/state identity, and privileged
  rules against atemporal finite compatibility histories.
- **Quantum information and quantum control:** channels, process tensors,
  combs, coherent/recorded histories, memory, intervention, and no-signalling
  controls. Do not let a convenient qubit factorization become ontology.
- **Formal and execution tools:** Julia for exact/native semantic variants;
