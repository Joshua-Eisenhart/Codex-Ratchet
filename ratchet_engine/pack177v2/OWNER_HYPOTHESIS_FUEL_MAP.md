# Owner-hypothesis fuel map

This is a faithful proposal map, not Ratchet canon, a selector, or a statement
of validated physics. Uncertainties are preserved instead of filled in.

## Foundational ontology and Axis 0

- Causality is rejected as fundamental. It may emerge as an effective machine
  description inside a protected, persistent region.
- Finite many possible future histories literally participate in constituting
  the present. This is the owner's noncausal/retrocausal reading, not merely an
  empirically equivalent narrative kept at arm's length.
- `KE iff PE` is an owner formula. This pack does not invent an expansion of
  the abbreviations.
- Feynman path integration is central, especially to Axis 0. The intended root
  carrier is finite: indistinguishable histories add amplitudes before records
  or probabilities separate them. An infinite continuum of paths is not
  required.
- Axis 0 is a dynamic allostatic-amplification versus
  homeostatic-reconvergence degree of freedom of the whole nested object, not
  a static XOR label or one terrain-local entropy scalar.
- The proposed entropy gradient is present from the beginning and drives
  exploration of every available escape. Nested constraint closes or
  internalizes those escapes. The exact mathematical definition of this
  physical gradient remains open.

## Literal proposed manifold and directional cosmology

- Ring-checkerboard recursion plus nested Hopf tori and possible higher shells
  is the literal proposed entropic-geometric constraint manifold, not a picture
  placed around some other carrier.
- `S^3 -> S^2` is one local Hopf rung, not a ceiling. Higher `S^7`, `S^15`,
  and other shell candidates remain open; their exact order and weld are not
  canon.
- Flat checkerboards, spherical charts, graphs, CA/QCA, spinor networks, and
  tensor networks are possible charts or execution grammars. None is allowed
  to replace the full nested manifold silently.
- JK fuzz is a finite real field of future possibilities on this manifold.
- `i` is proposed as the global shell-size/expansion-order clock. It is not
  automatically density-matrix entropy or a conventional time coordinate.
- Dark energy is what is coming inward: future possibility and expansion.
  Outgoing past/dark-matter record is not the source of expansion.
- Expansion supplies the push/compression associated with gravity. The exact
  finite route to Einstein/Newton behavior is open and must not insert the
  desired scaling by hand.
- Resolved past information is retained outward on shells as the proposed
  dark-matter/record face. This is distinct from incoming future possibility.

## Chirality, spinors, nesting, flux, and memory

- Chirality is cosmogenetic and selection-bearing across nested levels. It is
  not a late decorative sign.
- Left/right Weyl-spinor-like sectors, or another genuinely chiral carrier,
  are early high-value candidates. Each carries an oriented flux-like
  distinction; nesting provides the transverse region in which full geometric
  flux can exist and accumulate. The precise layer order is open.
- Spinors are preferred candidates where they generate vectors/tensors as
  bilinears with fewer independent installed choices. This is not a ban on
  vector or tensor representations and not a claim that qubits contradict
  spinors.
- Existing spinor-memory, quantum-Hopfield, and octonion-spinor-network work
  supplies distinct candidate memories: projective basin identity, lifted
  sign/holonomy history, and bracketing/associator history.
- Nonassociativity may be an additional escape degree. The Ratchet must compete
  genuine nonassociative carriers, free bracketed histories, weaker coherence,
  and effective nonassociativity produced by compose/compress/re-embed.

## Late terrains, engines, and unresolved 64

- Terrains are proposed late topological subbasins inside each chiral engine,
  with smooth transitions and entropy/geometry/flux jointly expressed. Their
  Jungian names are not ontological primitives.
- Each engine is proposed to have two distinct loop basins using the same four
  terrain families twice. Both loop occurrences are unique even when their
  terrain repertoire is correlated.
- Two engine types have nearly identical capabilities but different oriented
  placement/chirality. Their unusual paired organization is a high-value
  attractor hypothesis, not a generator target.
- Sixteen realized stage placements are proposed. The claim that each has four
  temporal substages is not founded. What the full 64 addresses compute,
  constrain, transport, or represent remains open.
- I Ching, Taijitu, Jungian notation, and IGT are lenses and proposal
  generators. Labels are arbitrary; no symbolic order or bit adjacency is
  allowed to become physical geometry without independent work.
- Axes were proposed as degrees of freedom of the constraint
  manifold/attractor, not the geometry itself. Later mesh degrees and the
  proposed 7-12 range must be earned from collective escape modes, not named in
  advance.

## Convergence and physics routes

- Independent evolution of ANS, PSNS, and SNS-like organizations motivates a
  universality class of recurring attractor architectures: different
  lineages, different survivors, correlated organization. It is not a claim of
  one unique terminal basin.
- The project may explore several routes by which its ratcheted mathematics
  could accommodate gravity and Standard Model structure: discrete spin
  geometry, Clifford/chiral modules, noncommutative or spectral geometry,
  gauge connections/holonomy, division/exceptional algebra, higher Hopf
  structure, and finite history sums. These are competing routes, not proof.
- A worthwhile result may be that different mathematical presentations become
  correlated charts of one constrained attractor. The Ratchet must still
  preserve countermodels and decoys and may return `HOLD`.
