# Separation contract

Three surfaces must never be silently merged.

## 1. Ratchet kernel

The kernel defines freezing, execution, measurement, plural MSS frontiers,
controls, Purgatory, residual compilation, recursive rerunning, and the pawl.
It is hypothesis-agnostic.

## 2. Exploration grammar

The grammar proposes finite mathematical candidates. It may include relations,
partial operations, automata, spinor modules, Clifford or nonassociative
algebras, cell complexes, coherent finite histories, JEPA-like predictive
quotients, or other typed families. These are search lanes, not truths.

The grammar is replaceable and frozen per tick. Candidate generation may use
provenance-tagged human proposals, but labels, analogies, and desired outcomes
cannot enter selectors, costs, gates, stopping rules, or measurements.

## 3. Owner-hypothesis reservoir

The owner's proposed structures are high-value seeds and sealed comparisons:
the literal nested entropic-geometric constraint manifold, finite coherent
future histories, nonfundamental causality, JK fuzz, the `i` clock, directional
gravity/expansion, spinor memory and chirality, ring checkerboard, nested Hopf
or higher shells, the two engines, 16/64 placement hypotheses, and related
mathematical lenses.

They must be preserved accurately. They must not be installed into the root
kernel or used as target leakage. A hypothesis can survive, evolve, recur, or
fail under the same process as any other candidate.

Leviathan is a fourth, nonmathematical-authority surface: it schedules work,
records events, maintains the context graph, resumes interrupted runs, and
preserves provenance. It never selects mathematical truth.
