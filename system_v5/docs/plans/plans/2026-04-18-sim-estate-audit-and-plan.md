## Actual Sim Principles

These are the sim-design principles, not the broad system philosophy.

1. One sim, one bounded claim.
2. One admitted substrate. Hold the carrier/support fixed while testing the object.
3. Name the exact math object or exact relation under test.
4. Every packet needs positive, negative, and boundary pressure.
5. A named tool only counts if it is load-bearing in the claim.
6. Local before coupled. Pairwise cannot substitute for missing local lego work.
7. Same claim, multiple pressures where appropriate: symbolic, numeric, topology, graph, solver, geometry.
8. Result labels stay separate: `exists`, `runs`, `passes local rerun`, `canonical by process`.
9. Artifacts are part of the evidence object, not bookkeeping extras.
10. No late-structure smuggling: no bridge/axis/engine closure imported into lower-lego packets.
