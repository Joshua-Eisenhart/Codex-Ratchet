## External Research Pressure, Not Proof

The external literature raises the bar in two directions.

First, noncommutative geometry already has a mature program where a geometric object produces Standard Model structure coupled to gravity. Chamseddine, Connes, and Marcolli describe an NCG-based effective unified theory for the Standard Model with neutrino mixing, minimally coupled to gravity, where spacetime is treated as an ordinary spin manifold product a finite noncommutative geometry. That is not your model, but it is a serious precedent for the target shape:

```text
one geometric construction -> gravity side + Standard Model side
```

Second, division-algebra routes already show that `R,C,H,O`, Clifford structures, ideals, chirality, `SU(3)`-like behavior, and charge tables can be extracted in nontrivial ways. Furey's work is especially relevant as pressure because it tries to derive Standard Model particle representations from algebraic action rather than adding particles by hand.

Third, Baez's octonion review keeps the non-associativity branch honest: octonions are mathematically rich and connect to spinors, Clifford algebras, Jordan algebras, and exceptional groups, but that richness is exactly why name-matching is dangerous. It supplies possible machinery, not admission.

Fourth, spin networks are not just a project metaphor. Rovelli and Smolin make spin-network states a serious quantum-geometry basis. That supports the legitimacy of finite spin-network thinking as a research lane, while still leaving this repo's carrier-admission gates untouched.
