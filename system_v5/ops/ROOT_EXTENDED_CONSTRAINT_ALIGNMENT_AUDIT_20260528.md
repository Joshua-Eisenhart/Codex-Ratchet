## Enforcement Rule Going Forward

Any new layer, true G-structure, or geometry-alternative sim must declare one of
these statuses:

- `full_bounded_candidate`: satisfies root constraints and the applicable
  extended constraints for a source-native layer or geometry formal scout.
- `root_adjacent_only`: satisfies F01/N01 and finite-map evidence but lacks
  source-native extended carrier/readout coverage.
- `adapter_only`: useful translation/support evidence but not a standalone
  layer or geometry candidate.
- `blocked`: cannot currently preserve the finite map, root constraints, or
  required extended fields.

No receipt may promote to stacking, flux, Xi/Phi0, Axis0, FEP/Holodeck,
physics/gravity, official G-structure selection, or final manifold admission
unless the lower dependency chain is cited and the blocked consumers are
explicitly opened by a later result.

Order and nesting tests have an additional gate:

```text
system_v5/ops/NONCOMMUTATION_AND_NESTED_LAYER_TEST_GATE_20260528.md
```

That gate blocks broad noncommutation-order sweeps and nested-layer result
comparisons until their parent layer/G-structure sims are complete under the
current extended standard. N01 local witnesses remain required inside individual
layer sims; they do not by themselves authorize cross-layer or broad order
testing.
