# Bridge Classification Policy

`bridge` is a runner admission class, not a scientific promotion.

Bridge sims are Python-runner sims whose filename or content crosses classical and nonclassical surfaces. They are not admissible as axis, QIT, GStack, bridge-claim, or assembly evidence from runner execution alone.

Operational rules:

- Bridge rows may be inspected, linted, and blocked by the normal queue tooling.
- Bridge rows stay on the gated side of the runner until a dedicated bridge lane exists.
- A bridge row can become queue-ready only with a v4.2 Wizard admission artifact and the same strict result contract as other rows.
- A passing bridge row is still local evidence. Promotion requires parent receipts for the exact tool/function/lego surfaces and explicit gate clearance.
- `sim_runner_taxonomy_audit.py --strict` may fail while unknown rows remain. Bridge rows are not unknowns; they are intentionally gated rows.

Current implication: the 962 bridge-class sims are a review surface, not a backlog that should be bulk-promoted or bulk-run.
