## 1. Contradictions or category errors

1. **Envelope vs. claim ceiling mismatch.** `runner_all_completed=true` and `capability_fit_all_pass=true` coexist with `promotion_eligible=false` and `all_pass=false`. That is coherent only if “capability fit” is defined as tool-chain execution, not scientific integrity. The story still risks treating completed lanes as partial promotion fuel when H is explicitly a semantic-contract gap, not a green integrity result.

2. **H category error risk.** The correct reading is “native contract does not require DAG ancestry → cycle accepted → contract ambiguity.” Any residual language that frames H as “integrity defect found and reproduced” or as “ledger proven broken” overclaims. Conversely, treating H as a mere cosmetic fail while keeping capability_fit green understates the contract hole.

3. **D vs. foundation language.** D is a bounded tool-fit chain with no PyTorch leg and no admitted scientific basin/manifold. Any narrative that places D on the same evidence ladder rung as “root basin admission” or “manifold convergence” is a category error. Tool fit ≠ basin admission.

4. **Scout vs. living chain.** Non-promoting formal scouts that show a noncollapsed Axis0 vector fixture do not repair the living foundation chain blocked at an empty Axis0 candidate bundle. Presenting scout existence as progress on the living gate is a substitution error.

5. **MSS unbound.** MSS is a separate finite classical quotient census. Until it is bound to the same candidate/state hashes that traverse basin and strict engines, “MSS recomputation” cannot be counted as a joined rung. Parallel existence is not shared-candidate evidence.

6. **Lev projection-only vs. end-to-end rhetoric.** The repaired Lev evaluator runs three commands and passes a scorecard with `projection_only=true` and non-promoting. Calling this “Lev execution of the campaign” is accurate only at L2–L3 tool/receipt level; it is not L4 verdict-bearing unification of Ratchet + QIT + applications.

7. **Evidence-ladder inflation.** Decorative checks, value-coupled recoveries, and self-reported tallies were previously flagged. Hardening addressed many concerns, but F/J/K still sit at fixed-fixture / fixed-network / fixed-endpoint-pair ceilings. Treating them as general engine soundness or as basin evidence remains overclaim.

8. **Three systems still separate.** Packet statements, provider answers, tool imports, local probes, and Lev wrappers do not establish scientific canon or a single running Ratchet. The story still lacks one candidate with stable hashes across the full path.

## 4. Focused attack: F, J, K, H, projection-only Lev

### F
- **Circular metric risk:** “Circular geodesic transport” may be value-coupled to the dynamics that named the endpoint pair. If the headline TV 137/160 or OT cost only appears under that metric, agreement of OTT and SciPy is still agreement on a possibly self-selected functional.
- **Ablation weakness:** Retaining 0/1 only where OT collapses to TV is good, but does not prove the circular metric is non-decorative.
- **Endpoint selection:** “Selects a named endpoint pair from the simulation dynamics” can be post-hoc pair shopping unless selection rule is hashed and frozen before cost computation.
- **Overclaim surface:** Easy to read as basin/manifold evidence; ceiling is fixed-pair tool fit only.

### J
- **Fixed 2-4-1 / 16 masks:** Exhaustive for that toy net only. CROWN/IBP containment + forged-inward rejection shows local soundness hygiene, not auto_LiRPA generality.
- **LP extrema:** Exact-region numerical extrema by LP can be tautological if the same linear relaxation family underlies the checker; need independence note or second formulation.
- **Decorative risk:** Enumerating all masks looks strong; if forged bounds are only crude constant shifts, rejection is weak.

### K
- **Fixed six-qubit fixture:** Full-support unequal-magnitude is a good probe, but “tool fit” only. Schmidt spectra + entropy agreement can pass while phase conventions, truncation, or ordering differ in ways the fixture never stresses.
- **Controls:** GHZ/product, tampered-state, units, dimensions are appropriate; still possible that tampered-state is detected only by crude norm checks (decorative) rather than spectral disagreement.
- **Engine coupling:** quimb vs ITensors agreement on one deterministic fixture does not stress randomness, distribution, or scale.

### H
- **Correct category:** semantic contract gap/ambiguity, not proven integrity defect.
- **Still dangerous:** Native accept of a cycle can be narrated as “verifier works as specified” (true) while the envelope’s `capability_fit_all_pass=true` soft-pedals that the specification is too weak for integrity language.
- **Replay risk:** Reproducing the cycle in packet-local purgatory may be a fixed demo packet, not a generative property of the ledger rules under mutation.

### Projection-only Lev (`executed_count=3`, non-promoting)
- **Scorecard vs. substance:** Three commands (campaign run, receipt validation, fabricated-claim rejection) can all pass while every scientific lane remains non-joined and H remains non-green.
- **Decorative validation:** Receipt validation may check schema/presence, not hash continuity across foundation/basin/MSS/engines.
- **Fabricated-claim rejection:** Necessary but low bar if the only fabrications tested are crude; does not prove the evaluator would reject subtle overclaims (“capability fit ⇒ partial promotion”).
- **Authority leak:** External or panel opinion must not be ingested as a fourth “command” or as evidence inside Lev receipts.
