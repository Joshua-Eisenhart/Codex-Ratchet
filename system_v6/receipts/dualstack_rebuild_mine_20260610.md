## B. Pinned joint object spec + choice points

Pinned object:

- Same finite carrier: scaffold says `SAME CARRIER requirement: one rho or psi_L/psi_R; never two engines on separate state objects compared by summaries` (`system_v6/foundations/working_math_scaffold_20260609.md:175`).
- QIT channel structure is mandatory: scaffold says `Without the QIT carrier (rho/psi, CPTP, unitaries, Lindblad, measurement/reset channels, entropy/coherent-information readouts, noncommuting composition, gap-erasing controls), Carnot+Szilard is only an analogy/classical control cycle.` (`working_math_scaffold_20260609.md:167`).
- Measurement object from pre-audit: `M` must be `a real CPTP channel on system tensor memory` (`support/preaudit_checklist.md:107`) and `a 4-dimensional input/output channel, represented by 4x4 Kraus operators or a 16x16 Choi matrix` (`support/preaudit_checklist.md:109`).
- GENUINE condition: ``M` is Choi-checkable as a joint CPTP channel and the post-measurement joint state carries nonzero system-memory mutual information` (`support/preaudit_checklist.md:155`).
- Coherent target: the audit compares source `M` to a `coherent CNOT-style fixture M` (`audit_verdict.md:10`) and says the failed object erased `|00><11|` coherence (`audit_verdict.md:38`).
- Szilard insertion: scaffold pins `with Szilard insertion I_Sz = R_M ∘ M ∘ Lambda_L ∘ U_H` (`working_math_scaffold_20260609.md:175`).
- Literal loop context: scaffold pins deductive `U∘E∘U∘E` and inductive `E∘U∘E∘U` (`working_math_scaffold_20260609.md:175`).

Rebuild spec:

- Hilbert object: `H_S tensor H_M = C^2 tensor C^2`, represented as a 4-dimensional joint density matrix `rho_SM`.
- `M`: a `4x4 -> 4x4` CPTP channel on `rho_SM`; accepted evidence is a Kraus list of 4x4 matrices or a 16x16 Choi matrix with TP/PSD checks.
- Coherence rule: the QIT lane must preserve the pinned `|00><11|` off-diagonal structure under the coherent measurement fixture; a diagonalized measured state is the classical-boundary lane only.
- `I_Sz`: realize `R_M ∘ M ∘ Lambda_L ∘ U_H` as channels on the joint object. Since `Lambda_L`/`U_H` are scaffold-level names but their exact finite matrices are not fully pinned in the mine sources, their concrete finite realization is a card-level choice point.

Choice points to preserve, not resolve:

- Whether `M` is represented as a unitary CNOT-style Stinespring/Kraus singleton, a broader coherent measurement channel, or another explicitly audited 4x4 CPTP channel. The sources require joint CPTP/coherence, not one unique matrix.
- Exact `Lambda_L` finite map and exact `R_M` reset implementation on the joint object. The source pins the composition name/order, not the only admissible matrix form.
- Whether headline `g_DI` is reported on the reduced system output, the joint object, or both. The pre-audit demands full-object binding for the proof layer (`support/preaudit_checklist.md:372`) and loop-level states (`support/preaudit_checklist.md:265-268`), but the formula itself is written as `rho`.
