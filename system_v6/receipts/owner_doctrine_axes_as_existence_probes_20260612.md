## The thesis (the root axiom's program-level form)

1. **Axes = the manifold's existence test.** Under a = a iff a ~ b, M(C) exists AS the stable
   quotient its probe families carve — there is no probe-independent existence to certify first.
   The existence criterion (operational, cumulative): a probe family is EXISTENCE-GRADE when its
   readouts (a) compute stably under the committed dynamics (neither trivial nor frozen — the
   axis0 standard 5d330b427), (b) are pairwise independent (the cross-cutting discriminators,
   identity-claims UNSAT), (c) recover across charts/realizations (the chart-recoverability
   program), (d) pass no-structure controls that genuinely fire. Each earned axis packet is a
   brick in the existence proof; the proof is the FAMILY's coherence, never one probe.
2. **The engine DoFs = the basin's test** (basin-contract requirement 8 in its strongest form):
   perturb along each engine DoF, READ THE AXES along the trajectory — return w/ reconverging
   readouts = the basin confirmed along that DoF; escape or readout-scrambling = the boundary
   found. Axes (what it is) + DoF perturbations (how it moves) = one operational attractor test.
3. **The contender-probe sweep is mandatory.** Probe-relativity is committed fact (the
   generating-set sweep: different generators, different partitions). A manifold read by one
   hand-picked probe family is cherry-picking. The round-3 pattern applies to PROBES: per axis, a
   registered alternative space (what else reads the same data), exact alias detection (probes
   equal up to reparameterization = one probe), light-symbolic pass then heavy teeth, co-survivors
   cited never merged, convention-pinned exclusions reopenable.

## Pre-registered expectations (falsifiable)
1. The axis0 readout survives its contender sweep as either unique-up-to-alias or one of a NAMED
   co-survivor set (a sweep finding dozens of inequivalent equally-stable polarity readouts on the
   same carrier would kill the "the Axis-0 readout" framing — then the axis is a FAMILY).
2. The DoF-perturb-and-read test finds at least one DoF direction with return+reconvergence (a
   basin row) AND at least one boundary (escape or scrambling) — a test that cannot find a
   boundary is not a test (the reachable-falsifier rule).
3. Probe-family coherence GROWS with each earned axis: the pairwise independence matrix stays
   UNSAT-identity as axes 3, 6, 4, 1/2 land. A later axis whose addition BREAKS an earlier
   independence row is a finding, never smoothed.
