## Classification Key

- `DYNAMIC`: perturb/evolve/read or repeated state/transition evolution produces a trajectory-dependent quantity.
- `ONE-STEP`: one committed update, channel, unitary, graph edge, composition, or finite schedule step family is applied; no open-ended trajectory response is measured.
- `STATIC-LABEL`: fixed labels, states, adjacency, tables, hashes, groups, homology, or state functionals are evaluated once.
- `STATIC-SYNTHETIC`: the measured field/quantity is invented or pinned rather than computed from evolving states; Axis-0 polynomial species.
