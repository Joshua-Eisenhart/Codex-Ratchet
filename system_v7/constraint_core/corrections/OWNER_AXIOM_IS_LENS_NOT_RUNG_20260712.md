The root axiom (identity is probe-relative) is how the ratchet's constraints are understood — the interpretive
frame for every gate, demand, and candidate. It is not itself a candidate on the rung ladder and must not appear
as a "tested/earned/killed" row in any rung inventory.

What IS on the ladder are structures built ON the axiom: the object-forming step (when do recorded distinctions
harden into object boundaries — tested: full grouping works but is nonminimal; probe-blind grouping fails), the
carrier (complex numbers earned via the axiom's continuous hidden-phase content as the forcing demand), and
everything above. The axiom does forcing work inside gates; it is never the thing gated.
