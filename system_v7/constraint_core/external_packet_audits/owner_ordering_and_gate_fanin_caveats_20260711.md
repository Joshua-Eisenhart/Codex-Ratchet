# Owner caveats: ordering is not canon; gates take mass fan-in

1. Cross-LLM results (webui bundles, this estate's own receipts) "can still be off" — treat every bundle's
   self-report as hypothesis until independently attacked here. (Demonstrated same night: the v0.4 L5 demotion
   shipped as an earned correction and was audit-killed as tautological.)
2. The ORDER of proposed things to ratchet is NOT canon. The exact ordering and all the sub-steps between rungs
   can be very subtle and take time to work out. Numbered inventories (0-15, 0-26, 14-layer, gate sequences) are
   routes through the dependency DAG, never runtime law. Do not freeze an ordering into the process because a
   bundle proposed it.
3. Each gate can have MASS amounts of options thrown at it at once. Wide fan-in at a gate is the expected shape
   (generate widely; strict admission) — a gate seeing only a handful of candidates is under-explored, not clean.
