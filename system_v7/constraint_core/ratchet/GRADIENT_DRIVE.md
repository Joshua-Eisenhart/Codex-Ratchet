Constrained distinguishability is the root. A tooth requires a witnessed gradient, but the gradient representation is
itself a proposal.

\[

\qquad L_D(\pi)=|C_D(\pi)|.

## Survival conditions

A drive proposal must:

1. be positive on a move that repairs a live lost distinction;
2. vanish when the relevant demand edges are erased;
3. survive admissible relabeling/relisting;
4. be computed from the executed surface rather than a hardcoded before/after pair;
5. identify its endogenous, external, permanent, or local status;
6. avoid promoting one scalar orientation to a universal law.

No surviving drive means `UNRESOLVED_GATE__DIG_CONTINUES`. It never means that all gradients are absent.
