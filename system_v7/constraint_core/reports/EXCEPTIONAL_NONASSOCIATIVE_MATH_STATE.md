## Entropic geometry actually computed on the branch

For cubic spectral values \(\lambda_i\), the finite spectral entropy is

\[
S_J(\rho)=-\sum_{i=1}^{3}\lambda_i\log\lambda_i.
\]

The J3(O) probe found basis-independence to \(2.22\times10^{-16}\) on its seeded F4-contained automorphism surface. A representative full density has spectrum \((0.38105,0.33501,0.28393)\), trace one, and entropy 1.09149.

The quadratic-representation flow was

\[
U_p(b)=2p\circ(p\circ b)-p^2\circ b,
\qquad
\rho_{t+1}=(1-\eta)\rho_t+eta\,\frac{U_p(\rho_t)}{\operatorname{tr}U_p(\rho_t)},
\]

with \(\eta=0.08\). Across 290 finite samples it preserved trace to \(4.44\times10^{-16}\) and kept the minimum spectral value positive (minimum \(1.28\times10^{-4}\)). Raw spectral entropy was **not** monotone. The epsilon-shadow Jordan relative entropy to the correct primitive fixed point was monotone-decreasing in the finite battery; a wrong fixed point broke monotonicity. A rank-two idempotent produced an attracting face, not a unique OP2 point. These negatives are part of the result.

The v4 Jordan DPI probe then tested one positive trace-preserving, non-block-scalar J3(O) instrument and observed

\[
D_J(\Phi\rho\Vert\Phi\sigma)\le D_J(\rho\Vert\sigma)
\]

on its declared finite grid. All 72 associator trajectories remained above the \(5\times10^{-10}\) floor. The associative Herm3(C) surrogate failed to reproduce the branch: maximum relative-entropy difference 0.06437 and maximum spectrum difference 0.12431. Automorphism-isometry and classical-contraction controls held; a deliberately wrong reference state broke the pawl. This is a finite scratch diagnostic, not a general DPI proof.

### The generalized dissipator negative

A fresh rerun of `jordan_dissipator_pawl_v2_sim.py` prevents a stronger claim. Its update combines a rotated Peirce pinch, an automorphism generated by \(\exp([L_a,L_b])\), and a trace-normalized quadratic filter without convex target mixing. On this broader finite flow, relative entropy to the computed fixed-point shadow is nonmonotone for both J2(O) and J3(O): the per-rung verdict is `pawl_fails` for each. Derivation/automorphism gates pass, and trace distance to the computed fixed point is monotone-decreasing, so the failure is not simply a broken flow.

Therefore the currently supported statement is narrow: a pawl exists for the declared primitive-idempotent flow, and DPI holds for one declared valid instrument grid. A general Jordan-dissipator relative-entropy pawl is **not earned and is contradicted by the present v2 finite diagnostic**.
