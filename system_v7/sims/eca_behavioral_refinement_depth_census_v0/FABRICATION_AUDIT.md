# Fabrication Audit

## Accepted

- The carrier, probes, actions, and depth convention are finite and explicit.
- Julia and JAX independently reconstructed exact transition and partition
  ledgers without peer-result reads.
- The closed-receipt controller compared every claim-bearing record.
- The maximum strict depths `3`, `4`, and `6` are supported on rings `6`, `7`,
  and `8`, respectively.

## Rejected Framings

- **"The perception engine works."** No learner participated in this census.
- **"The deepest fixtures are unseen tests."** The exploratory census selected
  and exposed them before any V2 preregistration.
- **"Three runtimes are necessary."** PyTorch was correctly absent; exact
  finite computation does not establish language-level intelligence.
- **"Depth six produces six engine stages or substages."** Refinement rounds are
  mathematical iterations, not QIT schedule positions.
- **"The dual ratchet derived four substages."** No geometry/entropy dual-ratchet
  derivation was executed here.
- **"The basin is universal."** This result is probe-, action-, carrier-, and
  boundary-condition-relative.

## V2 Admission Blockers

A learned formation benchmark must be frozen before training and must avoid
installing the answer through successor-pair edges or refinement-shaped message
passing. It must also have enough independent deep test families, seed-level
controls, a sealed evaluator boundary, and meaningful mass that changes after
the shallow baselines. The four depth-six size-8 witnesses alone do not satisfy
that benchmark-design requirement.

The next learned lane should consume bounded observations or sampled
trajectories, not stable labels, histories, paired-successor graphs, depths, or
partition hashes. Supplying complete dynamics can at most support an amortized
algorithm-execution claim unless a stricter causal-learning design proves more.

## Current State

The exact finite object factory is real on this bounded classical carrier. The
learned object-perception engine remains unearned. The proposed 64-stage engine
schedule remains a hypothesis, not a running system.
