# T9 Scientific Correction

## Killed Form

The deletion form of T9 is invalid:

```text
assign role R to runtime X
remove runtime X
observe that artifact R is absent
claim X has unique intelligence
```

The conclusion is installed by the role assignment. For the finite tasks in
this packet, Julia, JAX, PyTorch, and ordinary exact programs can in principle
reimplement one another's computations. Authority policy and receipt presence
are separate from computational capacity.

## Replacement

T9 is now **counterfactual evidence contribution and adaptive
replaceability**.

1. Freeze evidence roles independently of runtime names.
2. Remove one role while retaining every runtime.
3. Remove one runtime while permitting capable replacements to implement every
   affected role.
4. Hold interfaces, fixture secrecy, precision, hardware, wall time, memory,
   training budget, and scoring fixed.
5. Score scientific outputs, fault detection, held-out prediction, resource
   cost, and certificate strength.
6. Ignore missing policy authority, filenames, or receipts as scientific
   effects.

## Allowed Results

- A role may be nonredundant for a declared evidence packet.
- A runtime may show a scoped resource or reliability advantage.
- A diverse workflow may show measured unique fault detection.
- A runtime may be non-substitutable only within explicit resource bounds and
  only after capable replacements are attempted.

No finite run may promote these local results into absolute runtime-language
uniqueness or an engine personality ontology.

## Current Status

V0 established independent exact replication but did not execute replacement
ablations. V1 may measure evidence contribution and expose replacement tasks;
it does not preregister a green full three-by-three runtime/role matrix.
