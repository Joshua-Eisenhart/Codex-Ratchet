# V0 Semantic Audit

The v0 packet is mechanically useful but is not a forced Ratchet tooth.

Supported:

- exact finite tolerance/equivalence census through n=5;
- independent Graphs.jl, JAX, and PyG closure implementations;
- bounded free-relation z3/cvc5 queries;
- source/result binding and envelope mutation rejection.

Blocking semantic findings:

- the positive demand is selected to cross the fixed raw components, so the
  positive drive is wired by fixture geometry;
- MSS returns a real Pareto antichain but does not select the proposal;
- the demand relocation control is not simultaneous label equivariance;
- the policy has no post-seal held-out estate;
- no append-only before/facts/evidence/after pawl exists;
- mutations alter assembled envelopes rather than regenerating coherent engine
  receipts after source-level semantic changes.

This repair makes all five controls traverse the loss/decision functions and
makes the independent validator recompute the drive and MSS. Those are process
repairs, not semantic forcing. The current decision is
HOLD_DESIGNED_SURROGATE, with Ratchet state OPEN.

The replacement is the sealed preregistration at
../tolerance_to_equivalence_ratchet_rung_v1_semantic_forcing/. It requires
fact-only inputs, complete candidate enumeration, unique Pareto selection,
label equivariance, 256 post-seal held-out cases, coherent source mutations,
and an append-only pawl.
