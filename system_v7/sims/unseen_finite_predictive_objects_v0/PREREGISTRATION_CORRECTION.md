## Pair-Ceiling Correction

The manifest key `hard_negative_test_pairs` is retained to preserve the sealed
JSON. Its 16 pairs were selected by exact length-one and length-two similarity,
with longer-horizon divergence used as a tie-break. They are therefore called
short-horizon-matched test pairs in all result claims. No claim is made that
they are maximally difficult when the learner observes length-128 trajectories.

The frozen own-target preference threshold remains 0.75. Ease at longer
horizons is a scientific limitation to report, not grounds to replace pairs.
