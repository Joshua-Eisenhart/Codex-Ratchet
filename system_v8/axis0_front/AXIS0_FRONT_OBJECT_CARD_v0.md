## 4. Drive-coupling mechanism — what the doc documents, and the gaps

The task frames the target as: the drive as a foundational entropy gradient
("rising ceiling minus carrier capacity") that must couple into carrier/quantum
dynamics, because the honestly-measured v8 coupling is weak (see §6 mapping).

**What the doc DOES specify (two surfaces):**

1. **Shell-update Kraus rule (§9).** The documented mechanism by which the
   possibility structure moves the carrier state is:
   `rho_{r-dr}(x) = sum_{h in Omega_r(x)} w_h K_h rho_r(x) K_h^dagger` (§9).
   The drive couples through (a) *which* histories `h` are in `Omega_r` and
   (b) the *weights* `w_h`. The state is "the weighted field of possible
   continuations" (§9), explicitly NOT a scalar rate on a fixed generator. The
   compression compositor `C` "must not be silently replaced by a single argmax
   future" (§8).

2. **QIT-FEP instrument-history mechanism (§16).** Prediction/evidence/posterior
   is realised as finite quantum instrument histories `I_t = {K_(t,a)}`,
   `K_h = K_(T,a_T)...K_(1,a_1)`, evidence `Z_path`, and free-energy mismatch
   `F_Q` (§16). The polarity surface `Phi_QFEP_provisional = log Z_path + I_c`
   is provisional and the two terms "must be measured separately" (§16).

**Read together (§8, §9, §13, §16):** the doc's drive is the possibility-capacity
structure of the shell — `N(r,x)=|Omega_r|`, `H_Omega`, and `area(Sigma_r) ∝ r^2`
(§13) — and it couples into the carrier by *reshaping the admissible-history
ensemble* `{w_h, K_h}` that acts on `rho`, not by scaling one generator's rate.
That is the doc-grounded direction for fixing a weak coupling: put the drive into
`Omega_r`/`w_h` (§8–§9), where the doc puts it, rather than into a scalar rate.

**GAPS — the doc does not close the numeric coupling. Do not fill with invention.**

- **GAP-1 — weight law is unspecified.** §9 uses weights `w_h` and §8 uses
  compatibility weights `p_r(omega|x)`, but the doc gives no formula for `w_h`
  as a function of shell geometry, capacity, or a capacity gradient. Owner
  question: what is `w_h`? Candidate answering section: none present; §8/§9
  define the slot, not the law.

- **GAP-2 — "rising ceiling minus carrier capacity" is NOT in the doc.** The
  phrase and the subtraction form do not appear anywhere in §0–§40 (grep: 0
  hits for "ceiling", "drive" as a mechanism, "gradient" as the drive). The
  doc's nearest constructs are `H_Omega` growth (§8), capacity growth in the
  open regime (§12), and `area ∝ r^2` (§13). Whether "ceiling minus capacity"
  is the owner's intended concrete form of the §8/§12 drive is an owner
  decision, not a doc fact. Owner question: is the entropy-gradient drive
  `d(H_Omega)/dr`, an area/capacity difference, or the `Delta_r` components of
  `A0_raw` (§24)? Section that might answer: §24 lists `Delta_r H_Omega`,
  `Delta_r S_B`, `Delta_r K` as A0_raw components — these are r-differences, the
  closest documented "gradient" objects, but the doc does not name any of them
  "the drive".

- **GAP-3 — coupling from drive into the QUANTUM cut is unspecified.** The doc
  keeps `H_Omega`/`log Z_path` (classical/path) and the cut QIT observables
  (`I_c`, `S(A|B)`, negativity) as separate measured quantities (§16, §37) and
  warns the additive bridge "must not hide failure" (§16). It does not state a
  law by which a change in the possibility drive must move the cut observables.
  This is exactly the surface where v8 measures weak coupling (§6). Owner
  question: is the drive REQUIRED to move the cut, or is weak cut-coupling an
  admissible (even expected) result under §38 `scalar_entropy_only`? Section:
  §38 `scalar_entropy_only` implies that if scalar entropy alone explains the
  result Axis0 is not load-bearing — which cuts BOTH ways and does not settle
  whether strong drive->cut coupling is demanded.

- **GAP-4 — projection `Phi0 = projection(A0_raw)` is explicitly undiscovered.**
  §24: "The projection must be discovered. It must not be assumed." So the map
  from the drive-bearing components of `A0_raw` to the signed polarity is open
  by owner statement. Owner question: none answerable from the doc; this is a
  research target the doc deliberately leaves open.

- **GAP-5 — `i(r)` functional `G` is a candidate list, not a choice (§7).** The
  shell clock `i(r)=G(rho_Br)` has seven candidate `G`s (§7); the doc does not
  select one. Bears on the drive because `i(r)` is the r-order the drive runs
  along. Owner question: which `G`? Section: §7 lists candidates only.

**Verdict on the drive->quantum coupling mechanism:** the doc specifies the
*structural slot* (drive lives in `Omega_r`/`w_h`, §8–§9; QIT-FEP histories,
§16) but does NOT specify the *numeric law* that makes the possibility-capacity
drive move the quantum cut observables. GAP-1, GAP-2, GAP-3 are the unfilled
pieces. The doc-grounded fix is directional (couple through `w_h`/`Omega_r`, not
a scalar rate); the exact law is an owner decision.
