# Conditional candidate: spinor / QIT entanglement carrier

**Status:** FUEL only — one conditional rival for a code-driven ratchet.  This
document does not claim that its carrier, its layers, its order, QIT, spinors,
entanglement, or any manifold is correct, minimal, admitted, or physical.
Every occurrence of “layer,” “state,” “cut,” “carrier,” and “survivor” below is
packet-relative and finite.

## Conditional proposal

If a finite ratchet needs a carrier that can retain probe-relative distinctions,
make order-sensitive differences available, and change without importing a
pre-existing object domain, then a finite density-matrix carrier equipped with
minimal-ideal Clifford/spinor presentations is a candidate.  Its proposed live
gradient is the availability, on an explicitly earned finite cut, of negative
conditional entropy

\[
S(A\mid B)_\rho=S(\rho_{AB})-S(\rho_B)<0,
\qquad
I_c(A\rangle B)_\rho=-S(A\mid B)_\rho>0.
\]

This is not a root primitive.  It is a proposed readout only after a finite
joint carrier and a cut have been declared and tested.  Negative conditional
entropy is not treated here as an automatic admission scalar: it would need to
couple to a repair of a demanded distinction and fail an appropriate erased-cut,
product, and gradient-ablated control.

The proposed order below is a **schedule hypothesis**, not a ladder of beings.
The same finite candidates must be tested under fused, split, and permuted
orders; persistence means surviving those tested changes, not eternal survival.

## Candidate carrier

For a finite packet, propose a carrier family

\[
\mathcal C=\{(D,\,P,\,\rho_{AB},\,\mathfrak c,\,\Gamma,\,\mathcal U)\},
\]

where `D` is a finite set of demanded probe distinctions, `P` is a finite probe
family, `rho_AB` is a positive unit-trace matrix on a proposed finite bipartite
presentation, `mathfrak c` is a proposed finite cut, `Gamma` is a selected
finite Clifford generator presentation, and `U` is a finite population of
allowed update words or channels.  A minimal left ideal (or equivalent spinor
module) supplies a compact presentation for sign, chirality, and order-sensitive
probe actions when those distinctions are demanded.

None of the tuple entries is root-installed.  In particular, matrix positivity,
unit trace, complex scalars, tensor factorization, the cut, the Clifford
relations, and the update alphabet are candidate commitments to compare with
weaker and rival finite presentations.  `A` and `B` name positions in an
operational cut; they do not name pre-existing objects.

## Why this candidate might be the weak persistent-and-evolving region

Conditionally, this carrier is attractive when the active packet contains all of
the following finite demands:

1. A joint probe distinguishes presentations that matched marginal probes merge.
2. At least one action-order, sign, lift, or handedness distinction is live.
3. A retained difference must be updated rather than merely stored in a lookup
   table.
4. A signed joint-versus-marginal readout is tested as a possible driver, with
   `S(A|B)<0` surviving its own controls.

Under those demands, a density carrier is a compact candidate for joint finite
statistics; a spinor/minimal-ideal presentation is a compact candidate for
order-sensitive sign structure; and a cut-conditioned coherent-information
readout is a candidate for distinguishing a genuinely joint resource from a
merely local one.  The claim is only comparative: this combination **might**
presume less than adding geometry, a fixed spacetime, or a predeclared object
ontology while still offering persistence and finite evolution.  It has not
shown that no weaker finite relation, automaton, nonclassical probability-free
presentation, or alternate algebra can do the same work.

## Required rival and control packet

This candidate should not be evaluated alone.  A minimal finite packet should
include, where compilable:

- a finite relation/partition baseline with the same demanded probe edges;
- a finite automaton or transducer baseline with matched update-word budget;
- a density-only carrier without spinor/Clifford structure;
- a spinor presentation with the cut removed or made operationally inert;
- product and separable matched controls for every entanglement claim;
- cut permutations, cut erasure, and marginal-only reconstructions;
- action-word reversal, generator sign flip, chirality swap, and basis-change
  probes;
- fused, split, and permuted inner/outer schedules; and
- a gradient ablation in which `S(A|B)` / `I_c` is recorded but barred from the
  candidate-selection feature set.

The candidate loses its “weak” status if a rival preserves all active
distinctions, persistence, and update reachability with fewer declared
commitments.  It loses its “entanglement-gradient” status if the same repair
survives the product, erased-cut, or signed-readout-ablation controls.  It loses
its “spinor” status if a non-spinor representation carries every live
order/sign distinction at the same finite budget.

## Honest weaknesses and deliberately omitted layers

1. **The carrier is already rich.**  Density matrices import positivity,
   normalization, linear algebra, and usually complex amplitudes; Clifford
   modules import anticommutation and representation theory.  None follows from
   root constrained distinguishability without further work.
2. **A cut risks smuggling plurality.**  Calling positions `A` and `B` may hide
   the very object/subsystem distinction the root refuses to assume.  The cut
   must therefore be an earned operational compiler choice, not an ontology.
3. **Entropy is not root-installed.**  Von Neumann entropy and coherent
   information are selected formulas.  A finite coface loss remains the root
   process readout; `S(A|B)` is only a rival, conditional gradient proposal.
4. **Negative conditional entropy is not a universal fuel.**  It can be absent,
   fragile, direction-dependent, or irrelevant to the active demanded edge.
   It neither proves persistence nor supplies a physical arrow of time.
5. **Spinors do not yet require geometry, but may conceal it.**  A Clifford
   presentation can carry order/sign distinctions without a manifold, yet its
   usual geometric interpretation can sneak in rotations, metric structure, or
   spatial intuition.  Those readings are excluded here.
6. **Persistence cannot be declared from one state.**  Finite update words and
   a chosen persistence window are only tests.  They do not establish permanent
   memory, replication, attractors, or a global MSS.
7. **No physical interpretation is included.**  There is deliberately no
   spacetime, Hamiltonian, particle, gravity, cosmology, consciousness, or
   thermodynamic ontology layer in this candidate.
8. **Several plausible layers are deliberately omitted.**  No manifold/Hopf
   chain, metric/connection/curvature, QCA lattice, causal order, probability
   axiom, nonassociative algebra, universal tensor structure, external bath, or
   classical time parameter is installed.  Nonassociativity remains
   association-unspecified until a nonzero associator is required by a live
   distinction.

## Where this proposed order may be wrong

- A persistence/update witness may have to precede any cut or density matrix;
  otherwise the carrier merely stores distinctions instead of evolving them.
- A finite relational or nonassociative presentation may be needed before, or
  instead of, the Clifford/spinor layer; spinor compression may be premature.
- The operational cut may emerge only after durable survivor structure, not
  before it.  Conversely, a joint probe may be the very condition that lets a
  survivor emerge.  Both orderings must remain live.
- Entanglement/coherent information may be a late diagnostic rather than the
  live gradient, or it may be entirely absent from the weakest carrier for a
  packet.
- Geometry, if it becomes necessary, might constrain the spinor carrier before
  a cut becomes meaningful; the deliberately omitted geometric layer cannot be
  assumed downstream forever.
- Inner/outer nesting may not form a linear sequence at all.  It may be a
  mutually constrained finite update graph with plural survivors and
  order-dependent intermediate teeth.

Accordingly, this file proposes a falsifiable **candidate schedule**, not a
correct manifold description.  The code-driven ratchet should retain it beside
rivals, preserve its failures, and admit no layer merely because this narrative
is coherent.
