## 0. Stance and role

This document proposes, but does not establish, a finite density-matrix carrier
with a minimal-ideal Clifford/spinor presentation. Its proposed conditional live
gradient is negative conditional entropy,

\[
S(A\mid B)_\rho=S(\rho_{AB})-S(\rho_B)<0,
\qquad
I_c(A\rangle B)_\rho=-S(A\mid B)_\rho>0.
\]

The proposal is conditional on a finite joint carrier, an earned operational cut,
declared probes, and controls that show the signed quantity contributes to repair
of a demanded distinction. It is not a root primitive, canonical structure,
physical interpretation, ranking judgment, admission decision, or proof that
spinors, QIT, entanglement, entropy, or any manifold are required.

All references to layers, states, cuts, carriers, survivors, persistence, and
evolution are finite and packet-relative.

## 1. Conditional proposal

If a finite ratchet needs to retain probe-relative distinctions, expose
order-sensitive differences, and update a joint presentation without importing a
pre-existing object domain, then a finite density-matrix carrier equipped with
minimal-ideal Clifford/spinor presentations is a candidate.

The candidate makes three linked but separable claims:

1. A finite density carrier may represent joint probe behavior compactly.
2. A minimal-ideal Clifford/spinor presentation may preserve sign, lift,
   chirality, or action-order distinctions.
3. Negative conditional entropy may be a useful conditional readout of a joint
   resource when it correlates with repair of an active demanded distinction.

None of these claims is automatic. The density, cut, Clifford structure, and
entropy readout are candidate commitments to compare with weaker relational,
automaton, stochastic, non-spinor, and non-entropic rivals.

The proposed order is a schedule hypothesis, not a hierarchy of beings. Fused,
split, reversed, and permuted schedules must remain available.

## 2. Candidate carrier

For a finite packet, propose the carrier family

\[
\mathcal C=\{(D,P,\rho_{AB},\mathfrak c,\Gamma,\mathcal U)\},
\]

where:

- `D` is a finite set of demanded probe distinctions;
- `P` is a finite probe family with recorded outcomes and provenance;
- `rho_AB` is a positive, unit-trace matrix on a proposed finite joint
  presentation;
- `mathfrak c` is an operational finite cut assigning probe positions to
  `A` and `B`;
- `Gamma` is a finite Clifford generator presentation;
- `U` is a finite population of allowed update words or channels.

A minimal left ideal, or equivalent spinor module, is used only as a proposed
compact presentation for demanded sign-sensitive or order-sensitive actions.
`A` and `B` name positions in a tested operational compilation; they do not name
pre-existing objects or physical subsystems.

Positivity, normalization, scalar choice, tensor factorization, the cut,
Clifford relations, and update alphabet are all explicit candidate
assumptions. None is root-installed.

## 4. Why this candidate might be a weak persistent-and-evolving region

This candidate is attractive only when the active finite packet includes several
specific demands:

- matched marginal probes merge a distinction that a joint probe separates;
- at least one action-order, sign, lift, or handedness distinction is live;
- a retained difference must be updated rather than merely replayed;
- a signed joint-versus-marginal readout is a plausible repair signal;
- negative `S(A|B)` survives product, erased-cut, and gradient-ablation controls.

Under those conditions, the density carrier may provide compact joint statistics,
the spinor presentation may preserve order-sensitive sign structure, and the
conditional entropy readout may identify a joint resource unavailable to matched
local controls.

This is only a comparative hypothesis. A weaker finite relation, automaton,
nonclassical probability-free carrier, alternate algebra, or density-only
presentation may perform the same work with fewer commitments. No ranking or
admission follows from this document.

## 5. Required rival and control packet

Evaluation must keep this candidate beside matched rivals and controls, including
where compilable:

- finite relation and partition baselines with the same demanded edges;
- finite automaton or transducer baselines with matched update-word budgets;
- density-only carriers without Clifford/spinor structure;
- spinor presentations with the cut removed or operationally inert;
- product and separable controls for every entanglement claim;
- cut permutations, cut erasure, and marginal-only reconstructions;
- action-word reversal, generator sign flip, chirality swap, and basis-change
  probes;
- fused, split, and permuted inner/outer schedules;
- gradient ablation where `S(A|B)` and `I_c` are recorded but cannot affect
  candidate selection.

A rival that preserves all active distinctions, persistence, and update reachability
with fewer commitments weakens the proposal's minimality claim.

## 6. Probe / distinguishability test

The primary probe packet must be finite, paired, and held out from any carrier
construction that could otherwise memorize the answer.

For every demanded pair or edge `(x,y)`:

1. Run the declared joint probe and record its output, provenance, and update
   context.
2. Run matched marginal-only, product, cut-erased, and density-only controls.
3. Repeat with action words reversed, generator signs flipped, chirality swapped,
   and bases changed where those operations are defined.
4. Repeat under fused, split, inner-first, outer-first, and reversed schedules.
5. Record whether the candidate preserves the distinction, loses it, or changes
   its result only under a declared control.

A positive result requires more than `S(A|B)<0`. It requires a finite coface
contrast: the candidate must retain or repair a demanded distinction under the
joint carrier while a matched relevant control does not, with the difference
remaining after held-out probes and schedule changes.

The spinor layer is supported only if a non-spinor presentation cannot reproduce
every live sign, lift, chirality, and action-order distinction at the same finite
budget. The entropy-gradient layer is supported only if ablation of the signed
readout changes the repair outcome while the rest of the carrier is held fixed.

## 7. Predictions

- A joint probe may preserve a demanded distinction that matched marginal probes
  collapse.
- A minimal-ideal spinor presentation may preserve an action-order, sign, lift,
  or chirality distinction that density-only and non-spinor controls lose.
- Negative conditional entropy may correlate with retained repair only for some
  cuts and finite states, not universally.
- Product, separable, erased-cut, and gradient-ablated controls may remove the
  proposed repair advantage.
- A weaker rival may match all observed outputs without a density, spinor, or
  entropy commitment.
- Schedule permutation may reveal that the apparent layer order is an artifact
  rather than a persistent operational dependency.

These are conditional predictions, not claims about physical reality or
canonical structure.

## 9. Honest weaknesses and deliberately omitted layers

1. **The carrier is already rich.** Density matrices import positivity,
   normalization, linear algebra, and usually complex amplitudes. Clifford modules
   import anticommutation and representation theory. These are not consequences
   of constrained distinguishability alone.

2. **The cut may smuggle plurality.** The labels `A` and `B` may conceal the
   object or subsystem distinction the root refuses to assume. The cut must be
   earned by probe behavior and removable by control.

3. **Entropy is selected, not discovered.** Von Neumann entropy and coherent
   information are formulas chosen for this proposal. Negative conditional
   entropy is not a root process, universal fuel, geometry, time arrow, or
   admission scalar.

4. **The gradient may be epiphenomenal.** A negative conditional entropy value
   may correlate with a successful repair without causing or selecting it. A
   gradient-ablation control is therefore mandatory.

5. **Spinor structure may be redundant.** A density-only, relational, automaton,
   or alternate algebraic presentation may carry every live distinction more
   cheaply. If so, the spinor layer loses its minimality claim.

6. **Persistence cannot come from one state.** A single density matrix or
   successful probe does not establish durable memory, replication, an attractor,
   a global maximal stable set, or permanent identity.

7. **Finite statistics may be replayable.** A carrier can appear explanatory
   while merely storing a finite answer table. Out-of-sample probes and update
   reachability are required to distinguish mechanism from replay.

8. **No physical interpretation is installed.** The proposal includes no
   spacetime, Hamiltonian, particle, gravity, cosmology, consciousness, or
   thermodynamic ontology.

9. **Several layers are deliberately omitted.** No manifold, metric, connection,
   curvature, Hopf chain, QCA lattice, causal order, universal tensor structure,
   external bath, classical time parameter, or nonassociative algebra is
   installed. Association remains unspecified until a nonzero associator is
   demanded by a live distinction.

## 10. Witness / defeat conditions

This candidate can fail, and its conditional claims would flip, under any of the
following witnessed conditions:

- **Joint-carrier defeat condition:** matched marginal-only or cut-free controls
  preserve every demanded distinction that the joint carrier preserves.

- **Spinor defeat condition:** a density-only, relational, automaton, or other
  non-spinor presentation preserves every live sign, lift, chirality, and
  action-order distinction at equal or lower finite cost.

- **Gradient defeat condition:** product, separable, erased-cut, or
  gradient-ablated controls retain the same repair outcome, showing that
  negative `S(A|B)` was not load-bearing.

- **Out-of-sample defeat condition:** the carrier succeeds only on probes used to
  construct it and fails on fresh finite contexts, update words, or demanded
  edges.

- **Persistence defeat condition:** the apparent survivor disappears under the
  declared finite update window, schedule permutation, probe substitution, or
  deletion test.

- **Weaker-rival condition:** a rival preserves the same active distinctions,
  persistence, and update reachability with fewer candidate commitments.

- **Cut-invalidity condition:** the operational cut cannot be defined without
  importing object, subsystem, spatial, or physical assumptions excluded by the
  root frame.

These conditions do not invalidate the original document; they are tests of this
new v2 fuel-pool entry's own conditional claims.

## 11. Where this proposed order may be wrong

- Persistence and update reachability may need to precede any cut or density
  presentation; otherwise the carrier merely stores distinctions.
- A finite relational or nonassociative presentation may be needed before, or
  instead of, the Clifford/spinor layer.
- The cut may emerge only after a durable survivor structure, even if a joint
  probe helps that survivor emerge.
- Entanglement or coherent information may be a late diagnostic rather than a
  live gradient, or may be absent from the weakest carrier.
- Geometry, if later required, might constrain the spinor presentation before a
  cut becomes meaningful.
- Inner/outer nesting may form a mutually constrained update graph rather than a
  linear sequence.
- A rival may separate all currently demanded distinctions without any
  density-matrix, spinor, or entropy machinery.

Accordingly, this v2 is a falsifiable conditional candidate schedule, not a
correct manifold description, ranking, admission decision, or canonical account.
It remains `promotion_allowed: false` and `formal_admission_allowed: false`.
It admits nothing. The code-driven ratchet should retain it beside rivals, run
the declared probes and defeat conditions, and preserve both successes and
failures as finite receipts.
