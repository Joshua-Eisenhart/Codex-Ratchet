# Candidate Top-Down Manifold Description: Layers 12 → 0

**Status**: Conditional candidate hypothesis only. FUEL for a code-driven ratchet.  
**Not a correctness claim.** This ordering and these layer descriptions are one rival proposal. They will be compared against other candidates (bottom-up, middle-out, alternative carrier hypotheses, alternative schedules) by finite execution: population size, frontier cardinality under partition-refinement MSS, witnessed coface gradients, control outcomes, and whether the imposed backward constraints from high layers produce strictly weaker or incomparable survivors than rivals on the same demand families. No promotion, no canon, no ontology asserted.

**Shared frame (binding for this candidate)**:  
Root substance is constraint on distinguishability only. No primitive identity, equality, time, space, probability, or object is installed. Objects, carriers, geometries, and entropies are earned presentations that survive only as durable conditional structure. MSS = the weakest presentation that (a) preserves the active demanded distinctions, (b) persists under the current constraint set, and (c) can evolve when new demands appear. Nesting is mutual: higher layers constrain which lower mechanics remain admissible; lower layers constrain which higher compositions can be realized without collapse. All populations and evidence are finite. Entropy and geometry are one coface: the same unresolved distinction mass read geometrically (collapsed edges inside quotient blocks) and informationally (unresolved demands). Drive hypothesis (owner-flagged tentative): distinction-opening tendency (growth/expansion) versus distinction-locking tendency (records/locks); Axis 0 names the gradient between them. Gates, layer orders, and decompositions are hypotheses to be executed and compared, never presupposed.

**Stance of this specific candidate (live hypothesis, not truth)**: TOP-DOWN.  
Ratcheting begins at the highest object-forming levels. Axes 7–12 first organize Type-1/Type-2 engine differentiation and the construction of stable multi-object structures. These high layers impose constraints backward onto the admissible realizations of axes 0–6. Axes 0–6 are treated as deep shared mechanics that become operative only where higher layers have already selected and stabilized differentiated engines and persistent objects. 13 layers total (12 down to 0). The ordering itself is a testable schedule hypothesis: higher-layer constraints are activated first; lower-layer mechanics are admitted only to the extent they are required to carry the distinctions demanded by the layers above them.

## Ordered Layer List (12 → 0)

Each entry states the layer name, carrier (under this hypothesis), minimal presumptions, persistence+evolution rule, probe/distinguishability test, and this candidate's own honest weaknesses and order risks.

### Layer 12: Multi-agent IGT-scale interaction surfaces
**Carrier**: Finite registry of multiple differentiated engines (each carrying a full Type-1 or Type-2 chart) whose interaction outcomes are recorded as joint survivor structures. The registry is itself a conditional quotient: agents or shells are not presupposed; only distinguishable joint outcome classes under active cross-engine demands.

**Presumes (minimal)**: At least two stable, internally differentiated engine objects (from Layer 11) that can be placed in finite interaction without immediate total collapse. A weakness relation (e.g., partition refinement) defined over joint presentations. A demand family that treats cross-engine distinctions as live.

**Persists + evolves**: A joint presentation persists when every demanded cross-engine distinction edge remains uncollapsed inside its blocks. It evolves when new interaction demands open a tooth and a strictly weaker (finer) joint repair carries all prior plus new edges with positive coface contrast. Mutual nesting: Layer 12 constraints select which single-engine variants (Layer 11) remain admissible under coexistence pressure; single-engine variants constrain which joint classes can be nonempty.

**Probe / distinguishability test**: Execute the same finite set of cross-demand families once with the multi-engine registry active and once with all engines forced identical (erasure control). The test passes for this layer if the partition of joint outcomes is strictly finer under the differentiated registry than under the forced-identical control, and the contrast vanishes exactly when the differentiation demand is removed.

**Honest weaknesses, omitted layers, order risks**: This layer is placed first because the stance treats "object-making at scale" as the primary driver. Risk: the very notion of stable "multiple agents" or "interaction" may already require noncommutation, loop structure, or a drive gradient that have not yet been introduced. Omitted: explicit nonassociativity (emergence is expected later but not yet demanded). Omitted: concrete shell geometry (placed at Layer 9). Order might be wrong if pairwise coexistence cannot even be stated until lower mechanics (especially Axis 6 precedence and Axis 3 role assignment) have fixed action sides and chart roles. This candidate will be falsified for Layer 12 if code shows that no additional joint distinctions survive once single engines are already maximal under their own demands.

### Layer 11: Type-1 versus Type-2 engine differentiation
**Carrier**: Two (or more) full engine sheets, each a finite ordered collection of microsteps over a shared carrier, differing in at least sheet handedness, flux orientation (IN/OUT), H-sign, and the induced mapping from (loop-class, order, precedence) to stable placements. Differentiation is not a label; it is a measurable difference in reachable joint (A3, A4, A6) statistics under identical demand families.

**Presumes (minimal)**: A substrate carrier (Layer 8) capable of hosting at least the operator and composition structure needed to express one complete engine traversal. A loop-class and order vocabulary (Layers 3–4) that can be assigned differently across sheets. A weakness relation that can compare single-sheet versus dual-sheet partitions.

**Persists + evolves**: Each sheet persists when the distinctions it alone can carry (e.g., specific (outer=deductive, flux-in) versus (outer=inductive, flux-out) pairings) remain uncollapsed. The differentiation persists when a demand family that is sensitive to sheet flip produces a strictly finer partition for the dual-sheet registry than for either sheet alone. Evolution occurs when new demands (for example, coexistence with another engine type) force a repair that keeps both sheets while collapsing some intra-sheet variants.

**Probe / distinguishability test**: For a fixed demand family over stage outcomes or terrain placements, compare three registries: Type-1 only, Type-2 only, and both. The layer is witnessed if the dual registry admits at least one additional partition class that neither single-type registry admits, and that class disappears under sheet-erasure or H-sign flip controls.

**Honest weaknesses, omitted layers, order risks**: This layer is placed high because the stance holds that engine differentiation is part of object-making, not a downstream readout. Risk: Type-1/Type-2 may be downstream consequences of lower choices (Axis 6 action side + Axis 3 chart role + a drive) rather than independent object-forming primitives. Omitted: the Xi bridge and any Axis-0 readout that would give the differentiation a dynamical meaning. Omitted: memory and learning (Layers 10 and 7). Order might be wrong if a single undifferentiated engine already maximizes all demands that a dual engine can meet; in that case Layer 11 would be demoted. This candidate predicts that Type-2 rows will restore independence between certain lower axes (e.g., a4–b3) that are coupled inside Type-1; if code shows the coupling is independent of type, the placement is suspect.

### Layer 10: Agent / perception / learning loop persistence
**Carrier**: Finite fast/slow belief structures or equivalent survivor registries in which a fast candidate (prediction or projection) is tested against boundary evidence and a slower posterior over candidates is updated only on distinctions that survive the test. The carrier is the joint (candidate, evidence, survivor) presentation, not an assumed "agent" entity.

**Presumes (minimal)**: At least one stable engine (Layer 11) that can generate distinguishable candidate states and distinguishable measurement outcomes from those states. A cut or boundary demand family that can be applied to the generated states. A weakness relation over the space of candidate-to-evidence maps.

**Persists + evolves**: The loop structure persists when the map from candidate to predicted evidence preserves demanded distinctions across repeated generate–test–update cycles. It evolves when a new demand on held-out or counter-projection evidence forces a strictly weaker (more discriminating) update rule that still carries all prior distinctions plus the new ones. Higher layers (12, 11, 9) constrain which candidate generators are admissible; the loop constrains which generator variants remain stable under repeated testing.

**Probe / distinguishability test**: Run paired Type-1-style (candidate-first) and Type-2-style (evidence-first) loops over the same finite object cards. The layer is witnessed if the two method orders produce measurably different survivor sets or different held-out accuracy partitions, and both sets collapse to the same trivial partition when either the generator or the boundary evidence is erased.

**Honest weaknesses, omitted layers, order risks**: Placed high because the stance treats persistent world-model objects as primary. Risk: stable loops may require prior memory/record structure (Layer 7) or a drive (Layer 0) to supply ongoing variation; placing them at 10 may be premature. Omitted: explicit substrate implementation details and the physics shell interpretation. Order might be wrong if learning-like behavior appears as soon as a single engine plus a cut exists (i.e., Layer 10 collapses into Layer 11 + Layer 8). This candidate will be challenged if code shows that the additional distinctions earned by slow belief updates are already present in the raw engine traversals.

### Layer 9: Physics / cosmology shell-bridge structures
**Carrier**: Finite registry of possibility shells (nested spherical or equivalent finite-boundary presentations) carrying inward-compressing admissible futures and outward record shells. The carrier records shell-radius order as an i-like scalar (shell index or cut parameter) and the admissible refinement sets on each shell. No literal continuous space is presupposed; only finite distinguishable shell-order classes and their refinement populations.

**Presumes (minimal)**: Persistent multi-object structures (Layers 12–10) whose distinctions can be re-interpreted as shell-indexed. A substrate (Layer 8) that can represent boundary/interior splits. A drive gradient (Layer 0) that supplies the opening/locking asymmetry the shells are meant to mirror.

**Persists + evolves**: A shell-indexed presentation persists when distinctions between admissible futures at different radii remain uncollapsed and when record (outward) distinctions are carried without retroactive erasure. Evolution occurs when new demands on future-possibility compression or record preservation force a repair that adds or refines shell boundaries while preserving prior distinctions. Top-down constraint: higher object layers select which shell splittings are required; the shell layer constrains which object interactions remain admissible once finite light-cone-like boundaries are enforced.

**Probe / distinguishability test**: Compare a registry with explicit shell-radius ordering and inward/outward direction against a control that erases radius order (flat registry) or reverses direction. The layer is witnessed if the ordered registry admits additional partition classes corresponding to "future-compressed" versus "past-recorded" distinctions, and those classes disappear exactly when radius or direction is erased.

**Honest weaknesses, omitted layers, order risks**: This layer is placed high to honor the physical mirror (expanding universe pattern = MSS ratchet). Risk: shells and the i scalar may be earned much later as a readout of lower mechanics rather than an early object-forming layer. Omitted: explicit gravity or dark-energy dynamics; only the distinguishability pattern is carried. Omitted: the full Xi → rho_AB bridge (the largest Axis-0 gap). Order might be wrong if the shell picture cannot be stated until noncommutation and composition order (Layers 6 and 4) have fixed the meaning of "inward." This candidate is vulnerable if code shows that shell-like distinctions are already maximal in a non-shell substrate once Layers 11 and 8 are active.

### Layer 8: Finite substrate carriers
**Carrier**: Concrete finite presentations capable of hosting the operator, composition, and state structure required by higher layers (density-like registers, operator tables, interaction registries). The substrate is a finite grammar plus a compiler that produces independently testable behavior; alternative substrates (different matrix representations, different QCA rules, different graph carriers) count as distinct only when they induce different partitions under the active demands.

**Presumes (minimal)**: A demand family that requires distinctions beyond pure set cardinality or label shuffling. At least one higher-layer object (Layer 11 or 9) that actually uses the substrate's capacity to carry non-trivial structure.

**Persists + evolves**: A substrate presentation persists when the distinctions demanded by the layers above it remain uncollapsed in its compiled behavior. It evolves when a new demand forces a strictly finer grammar or a different compilation rule whose output partition is a proper refinement of the prior one. Top-down: higher layers dictate which substrate features are load-bearing; the substrate constrains which higher-layer distinctions can actually be witnessed.

**Probe / distinguishability test**: For a fixed higher-layer demand family, run the same schedule on two different substrate realizations. The layer is witnessed if the behavioral partitions differ and the difference vanishes when the higher-layer demand is removed or when both substrates are replaced by a trivial (label-only) carrier.

**Honest weaknesses, omitted layers, order risks**: Placed relatively high because differentiated engines and shells need something to run on. Risk: substrate choice may be an implementation detail rather than a manifold layer; the stance may over-weight "carrier tournament" evidence. Omitted: explicit nonassociativity at the algebra level. Order might be wrong if all higher-layer distinctions are already realizable on a single minimal carrier once the demands are stated; in that case Layer 8 collapses downward. This candidate will be challenged by any rival that shows equivalent frontiers with a weaker substrate grammar.

### Layer 7: History and record persistence (outward ratchet)
**Carrier**: Finite record structures (spinor-lift bits, density z-bits, outward shell entries, or equivalent) that carry previously resolved distinctions forward without requiring the generating process to remain active. The carrier is the set of preserved distinctions plus the probe family that can still read them.

**Presumes (minimal)**: A substrate (Layer 8) that can host both transient generator state and stable record state. At least one higher object layer that produces distinctions worth recording (Layers 10–12 or 9).

**Persists + evolves**: A record presentation persists when the distinctions it encodes remain readable by later probes even after the originating generator or shell has moved on. It evolves when new demands on long-term distinguishability force a strictly weaker (more discriminating or more robust) encoding that still carries all prior records plus the new ones. Top-down constraint: higher loops and shells decide what must be recorded; the record layer constrains which transient structures can be discarded without loss.

**Probe / distinguishability test**: After a finite evolution, apply a probe that can read only the record channel versus a control that must re-derive the same information from current transient state alone. The layer is witnessed if the record-only probe recovers distinctions that the transient-only probe has lost, and recovery fails when the record channel is ablated.

**Honest weaknesses, omitted layers, order risks**: Placed above the deep axes because records are treated as an object-forming persistence mechanism. Risk: record structure may be a straightforward consequence of any non-forgetting substrate plus a drive, making the layer redundant. Omitted: the detailed interaction between records and the distinction-opening side of Axis 0. Order might be wrong if stable records cannot exist until composition order and loop class (Layers 6 and 3) have fixed what "past" versus "present" means inside the engine. This candidate is exposed if code shows that all demanded distinctions remain available from current state even when explicit record channels are removed.

### Layer 6: Composition precedence and action-side mechanics (Axis 6)
**Carrier**: Finite ordered compositions of operators and terrains (or equivalent) together with explicit token precedence (which is written first) and action side (left versus right multiplication or equivalent). The carrier records the bilinear relation b6 = −b0·b3 (chart-role reading) as a witnessed constraint rather than an axiom.

**Presumes (minimal)**: Operator family and loop-order vocabularies (Layers 5 and 4) that can be composed in more than one sequence. A chart-role assignment (Layer 3) that can be paired with precedence. A substrate that can witness non-commutation of the relevant actions.

**Persists + evolves**: A precedence presentation persists when demands on action order produce different partitions for "up" versus "down" or "left" versus "right" compositions. It evolves when a new demand on closure or token identity forces a repair that keeps the precedence distinction while possibly collapsing some operator or loop variants. Top-down: higher engine types and records select which precedence patterns are required for stable objects; the precedence layer constrains which operator/loop combinations remain admissible.

**Probe / distinguishability test**: For a demand family sensitive to composition order, compare registries that differ only in declared precedence or action side. The layer is witnessed if the partitions differ exactly when the precedence demand is active and collapse together under commuting controls or precedence erasure.

**Honest weaknesses, omitted layers, order risks**: This is the highest of the "deep shared" mechanics and is still placed after object-forming layers because the stance treats it as selected by them. Risk: precedence and action side may be required to even state stable engine differentiation (Layer 11), which would invert the order. Omitted: full nonassociativity (the dual ratchet may force it, but it is not yet demanded here). Order might be wrong if the b6 law is an artifact of how Type-1 charts were drawn rather than a load-bearing constraint. This candidate predicts that Type-2 will loosen the a4–b3 coupling observed inside Type-1; if the coupling survives type flip, Layer 6 placement is suspect.

### Layer 5: Operator family selection (T versus F)
**Carrier**: Finite families of operators partitioned into at least two classes whose signatures differ under entropy/purity/contractivity demands (T side) versus orbit/spectrum preservation demands (F side). The carrier is the partition plus the witnessed behavioral split.

**Presumes (minimal)**: A substrate (Layer 8) that can express both contractive and unitary actions. Loop and precedence structure (Layers 4 and 6) that can host the operators in traversals.

**Persists + evolves**: An operator-family presentation persists when demands that are sensitive to dissipation versus orbit preservation produce different partitions for T versus F assignments. It evolves when new demands force a re-partitioning that keeps the behavioral split while possibly merging or splitting individual operators. Top-down: engine type and composition needs (Layers 11 and 6) dictate which families must be available; the family layer constrains which compositions are even expressible.

**Probe / distinguishability test**: Apply matched demands for entropy growth versus unitary invariance to registries that differ only in T/F labeling. The layer is witnessed if the partitions separate exactly along the entropy/orbit line and the separation vanishes under label shuffle or family-erasure controls.

**Honest weaknesses, omitted layers, order risks**: Placed after precedence because the stance sees operator family as downstream of how composition is allowed to act. Risk: the T/F split may already be forced by the branch split (Layer 1) or by the drive; placing it at 5 may be too high. Omitted: S-curve or lobe overlays as load-bearing until demanded. Order might be wrong if the entropy-carrying versus isentropic distinction is already settled by Axis 1 and Axis 2 before any operator table is chosen. This candidate will be challenged if code shows that T/F behavioral differences appear as soon as a drive and a branch are present.

### Layer 4: Loop traversal order (inductive versus deductive)
**Carrier**: Finite ordered sequences of generator applications that realize at least two distinct traversal directions (Phi_D versus Phi_I or equivalent) whose first measurable difference is the commutator term. The carrier records the order as a witnessed partition difference under non-commuting controls.

**Presumes (minimal)**: Operator families (Layer 5) and a composition mechanism (Layer 6) that can be sequenced in either direction. A loop-class vocabulary (Layer 3) that can label the sequences.

**Persists + evolves**: A traversal-order presentation persists when demands on sequence direction produce different outcome partitions for the two orders. It evolves when a new demand forces a repair that keeps the directional distinction while possibly collapsing some operator or loop-class variants. Top-down: higher engine sheets and records decide which directional distinctions must be maintained; the order layer constrains which operator sequences remain admissible.

**Probe / distinguishability test**: Compare outcome partitions under Phi_D versus Phi_I on the same demand family, with a commuting control that forces the two orders to agree. The layer is witnessed if the partitions differ exactly when the commutator is live and the difference disappears under the control.

**Honest weaknesses, omitted layers, order risks**: Risk: order may be an implementation detail of how engines are scheduled rather than a manifold layer. Omitted: the symbolic Taijitu assignment of spin direction to inductive/deductive (owner-reserved). Order might be wrong if directional distinctions are already fixed by chart role (Layer 3) or by the drive gradient before any explicit loop order is chosen. This candidate is exposed if code shows that all demanded distinctions are insensitive to traversal reversal once operator family and precedence are fixed.

### Layer 3: Loop class and chart-role assignment (fiber versus lifted-base)
**Carrier**: Finite loop presentations partitioned into at least two classes (fiber = density-stationary inner; lifted-base = density-traversing outer) together with an explicit chart-role assignment (outer = +1 or −1) that can differ across engine types. The carrier records both the geometric path distinction and the chart-role overlay.

**Presumes (minimal)**: A substrate and composition mechanism that can express paths with stationary versus traversing density. Precedence (Layer 6) that can interact with role assignment.

**Persists + evolves**: A loop-class presentation persists when demands on density motion or holonomy separate the two classes and when chart-role flip changes which class is treated as "outer." It evolves when new demands on role or path force a repair that keeps the separation while possibly collapsing some lower distinctions. Top-down: engine type (Layer 11) selects which role-to-path mapping is stable; the loop-class layer constrains which density behaviors remain admissible under that mapping.

**Probe / distinguishability test**: For a demand family sensitive to density change along a path, compare registries that differ in declared fiber/base assignment or chart role. The layer is witnessed if partitions differ exactly along the density-motion line and also flip when chart role is reversed, with both effects vanishing under path erasure or role shuffle.

**Honest weaknesses, omitted layers, order risks**: Risk: the fiber/base distinction and chart role may be coordinate artifacts rather than load-bearing manifold structure. Omitted: flux ownership (flux is treated as a control on loop class, not as axis content). Order might be wrong if chart role is already fixed by Type-1/Type-2 differentiation (Layer 11) before any explicit loop class is named. This candidate will be challenged if code shows that density-stationary versus traversing behavior is indistinguishable from simple operator ordering once Layers 5 and 6 are active.

### Layer 2: Representation frame (direct versus conjugated)
**Carrier**: Finite representation structures partitioned into direct (rho ~ rho) and conjugated (rho ~ V† rho V with transport correction) classes. The carrier records the isentropic property (dS = 0 for pure frame rotations) as a witnessed invariance.

**Presumes (minimal)**: A substrate that can express both direct and adjoint actions. Branch (Layer 1) and operator family (Layer 5) that can be applied inside each frame.

**Persists + evolves**: A frame presentation persists when demands that are sensitive to conjugation produce different partitions for direct versus conjugated classes, and when pure frame rotation leaves the relevant entropy or purity invariants unchanged. It evolves when new transport or conjugation demands force a repair that keeps the frame distinction while possibly collapsing some operator variants. Top-down: higher loop and engine layers decide which frames must be available; the frame layer constrains which operator actions remain distinguishable inside each frame.

**Probe / distinguishability test**: Apply a demand family to direct and conjugated versions of the same underlying structure, plus a pure frame-rotation control. The layer is witnessed if the partitions separate by frame and the separation is entropy-blind exactly for the pure rotation case.

**Honest weaknesses, omitted layers, order risks**: Risk: the direct/conjugated split may be a representational choice rather than a fundamental layer. Omitted: the interaction between frame and the drive gradient at Layer 0. Order might be wrong if the isentropic property is already forced by the branch split (Layer 1) before any explicit frame is chosen. This candidate is exposed if code shows that all demanded distinctions are already carried by operator family and branch without a separate frame distinction.

### Layer 1: Evolution branch (unitary versus proper CPTP)
**Carrier**: Finite evolution presentations partitioned into at least unitary (Phi(rho) = U rho U†) and proper CPTP (sum Kraus) classes. The carrier records the kernel distinction: unitary preserves spectrum and purity in a strong sense; proper CPTP permits entropy increase and contractivity.

**Presumes (minimal)**: A substrate that can host both unitary and non-unitary actions. A demand family that can see entropy or contractivity differences.

**Persists + evolves**: A branch presentation persists when demands on entropy growth or purity loss separate the unitary and CPTP classes. It evolves when new demands force a repair that keeps the branch distinction while possibly collapsing some lower operator or frame distinctions. Top-down: higher object and engine layers select which branch behaviors are required for stable objects; the branch layer constrains which operator families can be realized without immediate collapse into one class.

**Probe / distinguishability test**: Apply matched entropy-growth and purity-preservation demands to unitary versus CPTP versions of the same generator family. The layer is witnessed if the partitions separate exactly along the entropy/purity line and the separation vanishes when all generators are forced unitary or all are forced fully depolarizing.

**Honest weaknesses, omitted layers, order risks**: Risk: the unitary/CPTP split may already be implied by the drive (Layer 0) once any non-trivial substrate exists; placing it at 1 may still be too high relative to the stance. Omitted: the detailed Kraus history structure that would be needed for path evidence. Order might be wrong if the first measurable distinction that forces a branch is actually the operator family (Layer 5) or the loop order (Layer 4). This candidate will be challenged if code shows that all higher-layer distinctions are insensitive to the branch choice once a drive and a substrate are present.

### Layer 0: Distinction gradient drive (opening versus locking polarity)
**Carrier**: Finite gradient presentations that record an asymmetry between distinction-opening (expansion, new admissible futures, growth) and distinction-locking (records, preserved correlations, binding) tendencies. The carrier is the witnessed coface contrast between the two tendencies plus any polarity readout (sign) that survives under the active probe family. Axis 0 is the gradient between them; the chart readout (sign(cos 2eta) or equivalent) and any later bridge readout (Phi0 after Xi) are kept distinct.

**Presumes (minimal)**: A substrate (Layer 8) that can host distinguishable states. At least one higher layer (any of 1–12) that actually demands distinctions to be opened or locked. A weakness relation that can compare presentations before and after a candidate "temperature" or variation step.

**Persists + evolves**: A drive presentation persists when a positive live contrast exists between a registry that permits new distinctions and one that only preserves existing distinctions, and when that contrast is required by at least one higher-layer demand. It evolves when new demands increase the opening or locking pressure and a strictly weaker repair satisfies the increased demand with measurable additional contrast. Top-down constraint: all higher layers supply the concrete demands whose satisfaction requires the gradient; the gradient layer constrains which higher-layer objects can continue to differentiate rather than freeze.

**Probe / distinguishability test**: For a demand family supplied by any higher layer, compare a registry run with an active opening/locking asymmetry against a control that erases the asymmetry (symmetric temperature or null drive). The layer is witnessed if the asymmetric registry produces a strictly finer frontier or a positive coface gradient that vanishes exactly under the symmetric control.

**Honest weaknesses, omitted layers, order risks**: Placed last because the stance treats the drive as the deep shared condition that higher object-forming layers use, not as the first thing to be built. Risk: the drive may be required to generate any non-trivial variation at all, making every higher layer dependent on it from the start; the top-down ordering may therefore be inverted at the root. Omitted: the full Xi bridge construction that would turn the chart polarity into a cut-coherent-information object. Omitted: explicit nonassociativity emergence from the dual ratchet. Order might be wrong if the first tooth that appears in any finite execution already requires the opening/locking asymmetry before any engine differentiation or loop class can be stated. This candidate is the most exposed at Layer 0: if code shows that all demanded distinctions at Layers 7–12 can be carried by a drive-free or symmetric-temperature registry, the entire top-down schedule is falsified for the drive layer.

## Global weaknesses of this candidate as a whole

- Top-down risks presupposing "objects," "engines," and "agents" at high layers before the distinguishability constraints and drive that could produce them have been applied. The shared frame forbids primitive objects; this candidate tries to earn them as survivor structure, but the ordering may still smuggle object-like thinking upward.
- The 13-layer split between "object-making (7–12)" and "deep shared mechanics (0–6)" is a hypothesis, not a measured boundary. Rival candidates that interleave the layers or start from raw probe families at the bottom may produce weaker MSS frontiers on the same demands.
- Nonassociativity is expected to emerge from the dual ratchet but is not demanded at any named layer here; a rival that forces an associator earlier may expose an omission.
- The physical shell mirror (Layer 9) and the IGT-scale interaction (Layer 12) are both placed high; if either turns out to be a late readout rather than an early constraint, large sections of the order shift.
- All claims remain packet-relative and schedule-relative. Convergence of one finite population under this ordering does not canonize the order.

This document states one conditional candidate. It is fuel. It will be compared by execution, not by declaration.
